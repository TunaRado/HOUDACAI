8:00 AM - Early Breakfast

Fuel up for ancient exploration

9:00 AM - Colosseum Underground Tour

THE Roman experience - walk where gladiators walked!

  • Meeting Point: Colosseum entrance (check your ticket)
  • Tickets: €50-65 pp (underground + arena floor + Forum + Palatine)
  • MUST BOOK WEEKS AHEAD - very limited spots!
  • Underground (Hypogeum): Tunnels where gladiators and animals waited
  • See elevator mechanisms that brought fighters to arena floor
  • Arena Floor: Stand where gladiators fought - incredible feeling!
  • View from emperors' perspective
  • Main Colosseum: Three levels, holds 50,000+ spectators
  • Learn about naval battles (they flooded it!), executions, animal hunts
  • Guided tour (English) - fascinating historical context
  • Duration: 3 hours (comprehensive tour)

12:00 PM - Roman Forum

Center of ancient Roman life - temples, government, commerce

  • Included in Colosseum ticket - direct entrance
  • Walk the Via Sacra (Sacred Way) - ancient main street
  • See: Temple of Saturn, Arch of Septimius Severus
  • House of the Vestal Virgins
  • Temple of Julius Caesar (where he was cremated)
  • Curia (ancient Senate house)
  • Arch of Titus (commemorates sack of Jerusalem)
  • Use audio guide or Rick Steves free audio tour (essential for context!)
  • Duration: 90-120 minutes

1:30 PM - Lunch Break

  • Terre e Domus: Inside Forum exit, decent food (€20-30 pp)
  • Alle Carrette: Monti area, pizza and pasta (€15-25 pp)

2:30 PM - Palatine Hill

Where Rome was founded - emperors' palace complex

  • Included in Colosseum ticket
  • Legendary birthplace of Rome (Romulus and Remus)
  • Imperial Palace ruins - massive complex
  • Domus Augustana, Stadium, Baths
  • Best views: Forum and Circus Maximus from hilltop
  • Shaded gardens - nice break from sun
  • Frescoes in some preserved rooms
  • Duration: 60-90 minutes

📍 Ancient Rome Area

💡 Ancient Rome Survival Tips

  • Underground Tour: MUST book 2-3 months ahead - sells out fast!
  • Combo Ticket: Colosseum/Forum/Palatine valid 24 hours from first use
  • Best Order: Colosseum → Forum → Palatine (our itinerary!)
  • Audio Guide: Essential for Forum - ruins are confusing without context
  • Water: Bring refillable bottle - fountains inside Forum
  • Shade: Very little in Forum - hat and sunscreen crucial!
  • Bathrooms: Limited inside ruins - go at Colosseum before Forum
  • Wheelchair Access: Available but limited - check ahead
  • Skip-the-Line: Underground tour bypasses main lines automatically
  • Photos: Allowed everywhere - no flash

🏛️ Colosseum Fast Facts

  • Built: 70-80 AD under Emperors Vespasian and Titus
  • Capacity: 50,000-80,000 spectators
  • Size: 189 meters long, 156 meters wide, 48 meters high
  • Gladiatorial Contests: Lasted until 435 AD
  • Animals Killed: Estimated 500,000+ over the centuries
  • Building Time: Just 8 years (incredible for ancient times!)
  • Original Name: Flavian Amphitheatre
  • "Colosseum" name: From colossal statue of Nero that stood nearby
  • Damage: Earthquakes (847, 1349) destroyed much of outer wall
  • Today: Most visited monument in Italy

📚 What to Know About the Underground

The Hypogeum Experience

  • Only 3,000 tickets per day (vs 20,000+ for regular Colosseum)
  • Two levels of tunnels beneath arena floor
  • Holding cells for gladiators and condemned prisoners
  • Cages where lions, tigers, bears were kept
  • 80 vertical shafts with pulley elevators to raise animals/fighters
  • Trapdoors in arena floor for dramatic entrances
  • Sophisticated hydraulic systems (Roman engineering genius!)
  • See original Roman construction - 2,000-year-old brickwork
  • Very atmospheric - you feel the history
