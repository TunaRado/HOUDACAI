🎨 Milan's Greatest Hits

Must-Sees Today: The Last Supper, Duomo rooftop, Sforza Castle

Book in Advance: Last Supper tickets (ESSENTIAL!)

Walking: ~10,000 steps, mix of culture and exploration

Today's Itinerary

8:30 AM - Breakfast

Start your cultural day right

  • Hotel breakfast or nearby café
  • Pavé: Artisanal bakery near Duomo, excellent pastries
  • Pasticceria Marchesi: Historic Milanese institution since 1824
  • Try: Brioche and cappuccino

9:30 AM - Duomo di Milano

Climb the cathedral rooftop for breathtaking views

  • Combo ticket: €20-25 (cathedral + rooftop + museum + archaeological area)
  • Book online to skip lines: duomomilano.it
  • Cathedral interior: 40-minute visit, stunning Gothic architecture
  • Rooftop Terraces: Walk among 3,400+ statues and 135 spires!
  • Elevator to rooftop + stairs (€15) or all stairs (€11) - elevator recommended
  • 360-degree views of Milan and Alps on clear days
  • Take LOTS of photos - it's incredible up there
  • Duration: 90-120 minutes total

11:30 AM - Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II

The world's oldest shopping mall - architectural marvel

  • Walk through the stunning glass-vaulted arcade
  • Spin on the bull's testicles in the floor mosaic (brings good luck!)
  • Window shop: Prada, Louis Vuitton, Gucci flagship stores
  • See Savini restaurant - historic but expensive
  • Admire the incredible ceiling mosaics
  • Duration: 30 minutes

12:30 PM - Lunch

Traditional Milanese cuisine

  • Trattoria Milanese: Family-run since 1933, authentic (€25-40 pp)
  • Luini: Iconic panzerotti shop - Milan fast food! (€5-8, quick bite)
  • Must try: Risotto alla Milanese (saffron risotto)
  • Or: Cotoletta alla Milanese (breaded veal cutlet)
  • Pair with: Local red wine like Nebbiolo

2:15 PM - The Last Supper 🎨

Leonardo da Vinci's masterpiece - YOUR BOOKED TIME SLOT

  • Location: Santa Maria delle Grazie church (15-min walk or Metro M1/M2 to Cadorna)
  • ARRIVE 20 MINUTES EARLY - late arrivals not admitted!
  • Only 15 minutes inside (strict preservation rules)
  • Groups of max 25 people per timeslot
  • No photos allowed inside (enforced)
  • Security/climate control process before entry
  • Stand in awe of da Vinci's 500+ year old masterpiece
  • Look for details: Jesus's hand, Judas's reaction, perspective tricks
  • Visit the church itself after (beautiful Dominican church)

3:00 PM - Sforza Castle

Former Visconti and Sforza fortress, now museums

  • 10-minute walk from Last Supper
  • Castle grounds: Free to enter and explore
  • Museums: €10 (includes all castle museums)
  • See Michelangelo's unfinished Pietà Rondanini sculpture
  • Explore Egyptian collection and decorative arts
  • Stroll through Sempione Park behind the castle
  • Duration: 60-90 minutes

5:00 PM - Fashion District Walk

Window shopping in the Quadrilatero della Moda

  • The Golden Rectangle: Via Montenapoleone, Via della Spiga, Via Sant'Andrea, Via Borgospesso
  • Every luxury brand you can imagine
  • Perfect people-watching opportunities
  • Architecture and window displays are art themselves
  • Step into Armani flagship or Prada store just to see
  • Budget option: Just window shop and admire!

6:30 PM - Aperitivo in Navigli

Milan's canal district - nightlife hub

  • Take Metro M2 (green) to Porta Genova
  • Walk to Naviglio Grande and Naviglio Pavese canals
  • Choose from dozens of bars with canal-side seating
  • Recommended: Mag Cafè, Rita & Cocktails, El Brellin
  • Order: Aperol Spritz or Negroni Sbagliato
  • Enjoy generous free buffet (pasta, pizza, salads, cheese)
  • Watch the sunset over the canals
  • Vibrant, young crowd - very lively!

8:30 PM - Navigli Dinner

Stay in Navigli for dinner or lighter option if aperitivo was filling

  • El Brellin: Traditional Milanese, canal-side (€35-50 pp)
  • Al Pont de Ferr: Michelin-starred casual (€50-70 pp)
  • Pizza option: Ghe Sem - local favorite (€15-25 pp)
  • Try: Ossobuco with risotto (classic Milan combo)
  • Or just gelato and walk the atmospheric canals

10:30 PM - Navigli Nightlife

Experience Milan's vibrant evening scene

  • Bar hop along the canals
  • Live music at various venues
  • Browse the vintage and antique shops (some open late)
  • People-watching over cocktails or wine
  • Metro back to Duomo when ready

📍 Milan Highlights Map

💡 Milan Culture Day Tips

  • Last Supper: DO NOT BE LATE! They will not let you in. Arrive 20 min early.
  • Duomo Rooftop: Go early morning or late afternoon for best light and fewer crowds
  • Combo Tickets: Duomo combo ticket saves money vs individual entries
  • Photos: Allowed everywhere EXCEPT inside Last Supper room
  • Aperitivo Strategy: One good drink (€8-12) = access to all-you-can-eat buffet
  • Fashion District: Feel free to enter stores even if not buying - they're used to tourists
  • Navigli: Gets crowded 7-10 PM - arrive early for good tables
  • Metro: Clean and efficient - buy 24-hour pass if using multiple times

📸 Photo Opportunities

  • Duomo rooftop: Forest of spires with city/mountains beyond
  • Galleria ceiling: Stunning glass dome and mosaics
  • Bull mosaic: Your foot spinning on the bull (good luck tradition!)
  • Sforza Castle: Imposing fortress facade
  • Fashion District: Luxury storefronts and elegant streets
  • Navigli canals: Sunset over the water with historic buildings
  • Last Supper church exterior: (No photos inside the Last Supper room)

🍷 Milanese Drinks to Try

  • Aperol Spritz: Aperol, Prosecco, soda - classic aperitivo
  • Negroni Sbagliato: "Wrong" Negroni - with Prosecco instead of gin
  • Campari: Bitter liqueur invented in Milan in 1860
  • Franciacorta: Italian sparkling wine from Lombardy region
  • Nebbiolo: Full-bodied red wine from nearby Piedmont
