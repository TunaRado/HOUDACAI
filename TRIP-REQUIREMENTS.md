## COMMON REQUIREMENTS (ALL OPTIONS)

### Accommodation Standards
- **Hotels:** 3-4 star, central locations, breakfast included, cancellable
- **Budget:** $110-250/night average
- **Preferences:** 
  - Walking distance to major sights
  - Rooftop terraces/views when possible
  - Good reviews on booking.com/hotels.com
  - Elevator (after all that walking!)

### Transportation
- **Trains:** Frecciarossa (high-speed), book 60-90 days ahead
- **Metro/Bus:** Day passes in cities
- **Walking:** Primary mode - good shoes essential!
- **No car rental** except Slovenia option (driving in Italian cities = nightmare)

### Booking Timeline
- **Flights:** Book NOW (Dec 2025 - Jan 2026) - 6-8 months out
- **Hotels:** Book Feb-March 2026 - 4-6 months out
- **Museums/Attractions:** Book 2-3 months ahead
  - Uffizi/Accademia (Florence): 2 months
  - Last Supper (Milan): 3 months
  - Vatican: 2-3 months
  - Colosseum Underground: 1-2 months
- **Trains:** Book 60-90 days out when tickets release

### Must-Book-Ahead Attractions
- Da Vinci's Last Supper (Milan) - ESSENTIAL, books up fast
- Uffizi Gallery (Florence)
- Accademia Gallery - David (Florence)
- Vatican Museums (Rome)
- Colosseum Underground Tour (Rome)
- Borghese Gallery (Rome)

### Daily Budget Per Person
- **Budget:** €100-150/day (southern Italy, Slovenia)
- **Moderate:** €150-200/day (Florence, Rome, Venice)
- **Higher:** €200-250/day (Milan, Switzerland, Amalfi Coast)

### Food Philosophy
- Lunch: Light (€10-20) - sandwiches, markets, quick trattoria
- Dinner: Nice (€30-60) - proper sit-down, wine, multiple courses
- Gelato: Daily (€3-5) - non-negotiable
- Coffee: Multiple times daily (€1-2 each)
- Wine: House wine perfectly acceptable (€15-25/bottle)

### Packing Essentials
- **Comfortable walking shoes** (break in before trip!)
- Modest clothing for churches (shoulders + knees covered)
- Light jacket/sweater (summer evenings can cool down)
- Day backpack
- Power adapters (Type C/F, 230V)
- Refillable water bottle
- Sunscreen + sunglasses + hat

### Language
- English widely spoken in tourist areas
- Learn basic Italian phrases (locals appreciate effort!)
- Google Translate offline mode essential

### Money
- **Currency:** Euro (€)
- **ATMs:** Best exchange rates, use often
- **Credit Cards:** Widely accepted, notify bank of travel
- **Cash:** Keep €50-100 for small purchases, markets, tips
- **Tipping:** Not required in Italy (maybe round up or 5-10%)
