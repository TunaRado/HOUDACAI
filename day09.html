🎨 The Ultimate Art Day

Today: Accademia (David), Duomo Climb, Uffizi Gallery

Book Ahead: ALL tickets - lines are 2-3 hours without reservations

Walking: ~12,000-15,000 steps + 463 steps up the Duomo!

Today's Itinerary

8:00 AM - Early Breakfast

Fuel up for an art-packed day

  • Hotel breakfast or nearby café
  • Caffè Scudieri: Piazza San Giovanni, great pastries
  • Cappuccino + cornetto
  • Get there early - art marathon ahead!

9:00 AM - Galleria dell'Accademia

Home of Michelangelo's David - THE Renaissance icon

  • Tickets: €16 (BOOK ONLINE AHEAD!)
  • 15-minute walk from hotel
  • The David: 17 feet tall, carved from single marble block
  • Study every detail - Michelangelo was 26 when he started it!
  • See David from all angles (360° viewing)
  • Other Michelangelo works: Prisoners (unfinished sculptures)
  • Musical instrument collection (if you're interested)
  • Duration: 60-90 minutes
  • Tip: First booking slot (9 AM) = smallest crowds

11:00 AM - Duomo Complex

Climb Brunelleschi's Dome - Florence's iconic symbol

  • Combo ticket: €18 (Dome, Baptistery, Museum, Bell Tower, Crypt)
  • Book Dome time slot online - required!
  • The Climb: 463 steps, NO elevator, narrow passages
  • Not for claustrophobic or those with heart issues!
  • See Vasari's Last Judgment frescoes up close
  • Squeeze through the dome's double shell
  • Top: 360° views of Florence and Tuscan hills - BREATHTAKING!
  • Take your time, catch your breath, enjoy views
  • Duration: 45-60 minutes for climb

12:30 PM - Duomo Interior & Baptistery

Continue exploring the cathedral complex

  • Cathedral Interior: Surprisingly simple inside (dome is the star)
  • Baptistery: Ghiberti's "Gates of Paradise" bronze doors (copies; originals in museum)
  • Gold Byzantine-style mosaics inside baptistery
  • Optional: Climb Giotto's Campanile (414 steps, great dome views)

1:30 PM - Lunch Break

Rest those legs and refuel!

  • All'Antico Vinaio: FAMOUS schiacciata sandwiches (€7-10, expect line!)
  • Mercato Centrale: Food hall upstairs, many options (€12-20 pp)
  • Trattoria Mario: Old-school, locals' spot, cash only (€15-25 pp)
  • Quick lunch - save energy for Uffizi!
  • Rest your feet for 30 minutes

3:00 PM - Uffizi Gallery

One of the world's greatest art museums - THE Renaissance collection

  • Tickets: €25 (MUST BOOK ONLINE!)
  • Plan for 2-3 hours minimum
  • Masterpieces you'll see:
  • Botticelli: Birth of Venus & Primavera (Room 10-14)
  • Leonardo da Vinci: Annunciation
  • Michelangelo: Doni Tondo
  • Raphael: Madonna of the Goldfinch
  • Caravaggio: Sacrifice of Isaac, Medusa
  • Titian: Venus of Urbino
  • Tip: Get audio guide (€6) or download Rick Steves audio tour (free!)
  • Café on terrace with Palazzo Vecchio views
  • Strategy: See top 20 works, then explore what interests you

6:00 PM - Art Break & Gelato

You've earned it!

  • Exit Uffizi and decompress
  • Gelato at Gelateria dei Neri or La Carraia
  • Sit by the Arno river and people-watch
  • Process all the incredible art you just saw!

7:00 PM - Sunset at Piazzale Michelangelo

Best panoramic view of Florence

  • Walk up the hill (20-25 min) or take bus #12 or #13
  • Bronze copy of David on the piazza
  • Watch sunset over Florence - unforgettable!
  • See entire city: Duomo, Palazzo Vecchio, Ponte Vecchio, bridges
  • Street musicians and portrait artists
  • Bring wine or Aperol from a shop for sunset picnic (very common!)

8:30 PM - Dinner in Oltrarno

Explore the artisan left bank

  • Walk down from Piazzale Michelangelo to Oltrarno (10 min downhill)
  • Trattoria 4 Leoni: Famous pear pasta! (€30-45 pp)
  • Il Santo Bevitore: Modern Tuscan, great wine (€35-50 pp)
  • Tamero: Creative dishes, hip vibe (€30-40 pp)
  • Try: Pici cacio e pepe, pappardelle al cinghiale
  • Wine: Super Tuscan or Brunello di Montalcino

10:30 PM - Nightcap & Evening Stroll

Digest that art and food!

  • Walk along the Arno with bridges lit up
  • Cross Ponte Vecchio at night (fewer crowds, beautiful lights)
  • Negroni at Caffè Giacosa (where it was invented!)
  • Or digestivo (grappa, limoncello, amaro)
  • Return to hotel - tomorrow is Chianti wine country!

💡 Art Day Survival Tips

  • Book Everything: Accademia, Duomo climb, Uffizi - all need reservations!
  • Timing: Earlier = better. First slots have smallest crowds.
  • Duomo Climb: Wear good shoes, bring water, go at your pace
  • Uffizi Strategy: Hit the highlights first (Botticelli, Leonardo), then browse
  • Audio Guides: Worth it for Uffizi - Rick Steves free tour is excellent
  • Photography: Allowed in Uffizi (no flash), NOT in Accademia near David
  • Energy: This is a LONG day. Wear comfortable shoes, stay hydrated
  • Lunch: Keep it quick - don't waste art time on long lunches
  • Sunset: Piazzale Michelangelo gets crowded - arrive 30 min before sunset

  • Botticelli: Primavera (Spring)
  • Leonardo da Vinci: Annunciation
  • Leonardo da Vinci: Adoration of the Magi (unfinished)

  • Raphael: Madonna of the Goldfinch
  • Caravaggio: Bacchus
  • Caravaggio: Medusa
