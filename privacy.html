Privacy Policy

The short version: Mindrive does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything happens on your device. There is nothing to opt out of because there is nothing to opt in to.

Data Collection

Mindrive collects no data of any kind. Specifically:

  • No personal information (name, email, location, etc.)
  • No usage analytics or telemetry
  • No advertising identifiers or tracking pixels
  • No crash reports that include personal data
  • No cookies, local storage tokens, or fingerprinting

Audio & Processing

All binaural beat audio is generated entirely on your device using your phone's processor. No audio is recorded, streamed, or uploaded. No microphone access is requested or used.

Network Access

Mindrive does not require an internet connection to function. The app may use Firebase Crashlytics for anonymous, non-identifiable crash diagnostics to improve stability. No personal data is included in these reports.

Third-Party Services

Mindrive does not integrate with any advertising networks, analytics platforms, social media SDKs, or data brokers. There are no third parties receiving data from this app.

Accounts & Registration

Mindrive does not require an account, registration, or sign-in of any kind. There is no user database.

Children's Privacy

Mindrive does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. The app contains no mechanisms for data collection regardless of the user's age.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is ever updated, the changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Given that Mindrive collects no data, meaningful changes are unlikely.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to admin@houdacai.com
