🍷 Chianti Wine Region Day Trip

Options: Organized tour or rent a car and DIY

Highlights: Vineyard tours, wine tastings, medieval villages

Towns: Greve in Chianti, Castellina, Radda, Panzano

Option A: Organized Wine Tour (Recommended)

9:00 AM - Tour Pickup

Small group wine tour (8-15 people)

  • Pickup from Florence hotel or meeting point
  • Tour companies: Walkabout Florence, Tuscany in Tour
  • Cost: €80-120 pp (includes transport, 2-3 winery visits, lunch)
  • AC van or mini-bus
  • English-speaking guide

10:00 AM - First Winery Visit

Tour Chianti Classico vineyard and cellars

  • Walk through vineyards with guide
  • Learn about Chianti Classico DOCG production
  • See fermentation tanks and aging barrels
  • Taste 3-4 wines (Chianti, Riserva, Super Tuscan, Vin Santo)
  • Pair with local cheeses, salami, olive oil
  • Duration: 90 minutes

12:00 PM - Medieval Hill Town

Greve in Chianti or Castellina in Chianti

  • Explore charming medieval village
  • Visit local market square
  • Browse artisan shops and wine stores
  • See Chianti Classico consortium (black rooster symbol)
  • Photo opportunities of Tuscan countryside
  • Free time to wander (30-45 minutes)

1:00 PM - Tuscan Lunch

Multi-course meal at countryside trattoria or agriturismo

  • Often included in tour price
  • Antipasti: Crostini, bruschetta, salumi, cheeses
  • Primi: Pappardelle al cinghiale or pici pasta
  • Secondi: Bistecca alla fiorentina or roasted meats
  • Contorni: White beans, roasted vegetables
  • Wine pairings throughout
  • Dessert: Cantuccini with Vin Santo
  • Duration: 90-120 minutes (Italian pace!)

3:30 PM - Second Winery Visit

Different style winery or castle estate

  • Possibly a castle winery (Castello di Verrazzano, Castello di Volpaia)
  • Tour historic cellars
  • Taste premium wines and reserves
  • Learn to distinguish Chianti from Super Tuscans
  • Purchase opportunity (prices vary, €12-50+ per bottle)
  • Some tours include olive oil tasting

5:30 PM - Return to Florence

Scenic drive back through Tuscan countryside

  • Pass rolling hills, cypress trees, vineyards
  • Sunset views (if clear)
  • Drop-off at Florence hotel/meeting point
  • Arrive back around 6:30-7 PM

Option B: Self-Drive (If You Want Freedom)

Requirements & Considerations

  • International Driving Permit: Required in Italy!
  • Car Rental: €50-80/day for small car
  • Winery Bookings: Reserve tastings in advance
  • Designated Driver: One person can't taste wine!
  • GPS: Essential - rural roads can be confusing
  • ZTL Zones: Avoid Limited Traffic Zones in towns (huge fines!)
  • Parking: Can be challenging in hill towns

Recommended DIY Route

  • Pickup car in Florence (outside ZTL zone)
  • Drive SR222 (Chiantigiana road) - most scenic route
  • Stop 1: Greve in Chianti (town + winery)
  • Stop 2: Panzano (Dario Cecchini butcher shop if you're a meat lover!)
  • Stop 3: Castello di Volpaia (castle + winery)
  • Lunch in Radda or Castellina
  • Return to Florence via SR222 or A1 highway

Evening in Florence

7:00 PM - Rest & Freshen Up

After wine country adventure

  • Return to hotel and decompress
  • Shower and change
  • You likely had huge lunch - light dinner tonight!

8:00 PM - Light Dinner or Aperitivo

Keep it casual after big lunch

  • Aperitivo Only: SE·STO on Arno, rooftop views
  • Pizza: Gustapizza or Gusta Osteria
  • Light Bites: Obicà Mozzarella Bar
  • Or just gelato and evening stroll!

9:00 PM - Farewell Florence Evening

Last night in the Renaissance city

  • Walk along the Arno one final time
  • Visit Ponte Vecchio at night (beautifully lit)
  • Piazza della Signoria by night - romantic
  • Final gelato at favorite gelateria
  • Pack tonight! Train to Rome tomorrow
  • Hotel checkout at 11 AM, train around 12:30-1 PM

💡 Chianti Day Tips

  • Tour vs DIY: Tour is MUCH easier - no driving drunk, bookings handled
  • Book Early: Small group tours fill up in peak season
  • What to Wear: Casual but nice - you'll be at wineries
  • Sun Protection: Hat and sunscreen - lots of time outdoors
  • Lunch Size: Tuscan lunches are HUGE - pace yourself!
  • Wine Buying: Wineries ship internationally if you buy a case
  • Tasting Etiquette: Spit buckets provided - OK to use!
  • Vin Santo: Sweet dessert wine - try it with cantuccini biscotti
  • Photos: Rolling hills and cypress trees - Tuscan postcard perfection!

🍷 Chianti Wine Guide

  • Chianti: Lighter, everyday drinking wine (€10-15)
  • Chianti Classico: From heart of region, black rooster symbol (€15-30)
  • Chianti Classico Riserva: Aged longer, more complex (€25-50)
  • Gran Selezione: Top tier Chianti (€40-80+)
  • Super Tuscan: Not traditional Chianti (no DOCG), but often excellent (€30-100+)
  • Vin Santo: Sweet dessert wine, aged in small barrels (€20-40)
  • Main Grape: Sangiovese (must be 80% in Chianti Classico)

🍖 Dario Cecchini - The Rock Star Butcher

If You're a Meat Lover...

Panzano's famous butcher shop - a Chianti institution!

  • Location: Antica Macelleria Cecchini, Panzano in Chianti
  • Dario is a larger-than-life character (literally recites Dante!)
  • See his shop filled with hanging meats and poetry
  • Book dinner at his restaurants (Solociccia, Mac Dario, Officina della Bistecca)
  • Famous for his "Free Chianti" sign and theatrical personality
  • Solociccia: Fixed menu, no steak! (€30-40 pp)
  • Officina: Bistecca fiorentina experience (€50 pp)
