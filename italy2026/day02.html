🏛️ Venice - The Floating City

Hotel: Hotel Antiche Figure (Grand Canal views)

Neighborhood: San Polo, near Rialto Bridge

Weather: June average 75°F (24°C), sunny with occasional clouds

Today's Focus: Arrival, check-in, gentle exploration to beat jet lag

Today's Itinerary

9:00 AM - Arrival at Venice Marco Polo Airport

Welcome to Italy! Clear customs and collect luggage.

  • Currency exchange or ATM for euros (ATM usually better rates)
  • Purchase vaporetto (water bus) tickets at airport
  • Option 1: Alilaguna water bus to Venice (~€15, 1hr scenic ride)
  • Option 2: Water taxi (€110-120, faster, direct to hotel)

10:30 AM - First Vaporetto Ride

Your first magical moments on the Grand Canal! Take the Alilaguna Blue Line or ACTV water bus.

  • Watch palazzos glide by as you cruise the canal
  • Take photos of the stunning architecture
  • Spot the Rialto Bridge in the distance

12:00 PM - Hotel Check-In

Hotel Antiche Figure - San Polo district, steps from Rialto

  • Store luggage if room not ready
  • Get recommendations from concierge
  • Pick up free maps and water bus routes
  • Rest for 30-60 minutes if needed

1:00 PM - First Venetian Lunch

Light meal near hotel - try a traditional bacaro (wine bar) for cicchetti

  • All'Arco - Famous cicchetti near Rialto Market
  • Cantina Do Spade - Historic spot, great local atmosphere
  • Order: Prosecco and small plates (€15-25 per person)
  • Try: Baccalà mantecato (creamy cod), polpette (meatballs)

2:30 PM - Rialto Bridge & Market

Explore Venice's most famous bridge and the vibrant Rialto Market area

  • Walk across the iconic Rialto Bridge
  • Browse the colorful Rialto Market (closes around 2 PM)
  • Wander through the narrow streets of San Polo
  • Visit Campo San Giacomo di Rialto (oldest church area)

4:00 PM - St. Mark's Square (Piazza San Marco)

Walk or take vaporetto to Venice's main square

  • Marvel at St. Mark's Basilica (exterior today)
  • See the Doge's Palace from outside
  • Watch the gondolas at the waterfront
  • Listen to the café orchestras (no need to sit, just enjoy!)
  • Explore the arcades and luxury shops

6:00 PM - Sunset Stroll

Get lost in Venice's magical alleyways (this is the best way to explore!)

  • Wander through Castello or Cannaregio districts
  • Cross hidden bridges and discover quiet campos
  • Watch the golden hour light on the canals
  • Find a canal-side spot for aperitivo

7:30 PM - Dinner

Traditional Venetian dinner - book ahead or arrive early!

  • Osteria alla Staffa - Local favorite, great seafood
  • Antiche Carampane - Traditional cuisine, hidden gem
  • Must try: Sarde in saor (sweet & sour sardines)
  • Pasta: Bigoli in salsa or seafood risotto
  • Main: Fresh grilled fish or Fegato alla veneziana
  • Budget: €40-60 per person with wine

9:30 PM - Evening Walk

Venice at night is pure magic - enjoy a digestivo stroll

  • Walk back toward hotel via different route
  • See St. Mark's Square illuminated
  • Enjoy gelato from a recommended gelateria
  • Early night to recover from travel (aim for 10:30-11 PM bedtime)

📍 Venice Map

💡 Essential Venice Tips

  • Getting Around: Walk! Venice is best explored on foot. Vaporetto for longer distances.
  • Vaporetto Pass: Consider 72-hour pass (€40) - unlimited water bus rides
  • Getting Lost: It's part of the Venice experience! Yellow signs point to major landmarks
  • Water: Bring refillable bottle - public fountains everywhere (safe to drink)
  • Avoid Tourist Traps: Restaurants near San Marco are overpriced. Walk 5-10 minutes away
  • Jet Lag: Stay awake until at least 9 PM local time. Sunlight and walking help!
  • Cash: Many small places still cash-only. Have €50-100 in small bills
  • Dress Code: Shoulders and knees covered for churches (bring a light scarf)

🍝 Food & Drink Recommendations

Must-Try Venetian Specialties

  • Cicchetti: Venetian tapas - small bites at bacari (wine bars)
  • Sarde in Saor: Sweet and sour sardines with onions and raisins
  • Baccalà Mantecato: Creamy whipped cod spread
  • Risotto al Nero di Seppia: Squid ink risotto (black but delicious!)
  • Fegato alla Veneziana: Venetian-style liver with onions
  • Spritz: Aperol or Campari spritz - the classic Venetian aperitif
  • Prosecco: From nearby Veneto region - enjoy it everywhere!

📸 Photo Opportunities

  • Rialto Bridge from the Grand Canal
  • Colorful houses along the canals
  • St. Mark's Square from the waterfront
  • Gondolas moored at sunset
  • Hidden bridges and narrow alleyways
  • Grand Canal from your hotel window!
