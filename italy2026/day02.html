🏛️ Rome - The Eternal City (Take It Easy!)

Hotel: Hotel Artemide (Rooftop bar, near Termini)

Philosophy: NO intense sightseeing today! Recover from jet lag

Weather: Summer average 80-85°F (27-29°C), sunny and hot

Today's Focus: Arrival, check-in, gentle exploration to beat jet lag

Today's Relaxed Itinerary

9:00-11:00 AM - Arrival at Rome Fiumicino Airport (FCO)

Welcome to Italia! Take your time clearing customs.

  • ATM for euros (better rates than exchange booths)
  • SIM card if needed (TIM or Vodafone kiosks)
  • Leonardo Express train to Termini Station (€14, 32 minutes)
  • Or private transfer if you're tired (~€50, door-to-door)

11:30 AM - Hotel Check-In

Hotel Artemide - Via Nazionale, 5 min walk from Termini

  • Drop bags (room may not be ready)
  • Get local recommendations from concierge
  • Quick refresh in lobby if needed
  • DON'T nap yet! Stay awake until at least 8 PM

12:30 PM - First Roman Lunch

Light lunch nearby - nothing too heavy, you're tired!

  • Mercato Centrale Roma (at Termini) - Food hall, many options
  • Flavio al Velavevodetto - 15 min walk, great Roman classics
  • Keep it simple: Cacio e pepe or carbonara + water
  • Espresso to help stay awake!
  • Budget: €15-25 per person

2:00 PM - Easy Sightseeing: Trevi Fountain & Spanish Steps

Close to hotel, not too strenuous - perfect for today

  • Walk to Trevi Fountain (15 min from hotel)
  • Toss coin in fountain (over left shoulder!)
  • Grab gelato at nearby gelateria
  • Stroll to Spanish Steps (10 min walk)
  • People-watch at Piazza di Spagna
  • Browse Via Condotti if you like shopping

4:00 PM - Back to Hotel: Shower & Short Rest

Room should be ready now - refresh but DON'T sleep long!

  • Shower and change clothes
  • Power nap MAX 30 minutes (set alarm!)
  • Unpack and organize
  • Charge devices
  • Plan tomorrow's activities

5:30 PM - Rooftop Aperitivo

Hotel Artemide's rooftop bar or nearby spot

  • Enjoy sunset views over Rome
  • Order Aperol Spritz or Negroni
  • Light snacks/nuts provided
  • Relax and soak in the moment - you're in ROME!

7:30 PM - Easy Dinner

Nothing fancy tonight - casual, close to hotel

  • Trattoria Monti - Excellent Roman food, relaxed vibe
  • Ai Tre Scalini - Local favorite near Monti district
  • Order: Carbonara or Amatriciana (Rome's pasta kings)
  • Glass of house wine
  • Skip dessert if you're fading - get gelato on walk back
  • Budget: €30-45 per person

9:00 PM - Gentle Evening Stroll

Stay awake a bit longer to adjust to timezone!

  • Walk through Monti neighborhood (charming, local vibe)
  • Gelato from Fatamorgana or Come il Latte
  • See the Colosseum lit up at night (just exterior, 10 min walk)
  • Early to bed around 10-10:30 PM

💡 Jet Lag Recovery Tips

  • Stay Awake: Push through until at least 9 PM local time - this resets your clock fastest
  • Sunlight: Get outside! Natural light helps your body adjust
  • Light Meals: Don't overeat today - your digestive system is confused too
  • Hydrate: Drink lots of water on the plane and throughout today
  • Avoid Alcohol: Well, we're in Italy... but keep it light today
  • Walk: Movement helps - don't just collapse in hotel room
  • Tomorrow: You'll wake up early (jet lag!) - embrace it! See Rome at sunrise

🏛️ Rome Basics for Tomorrow

  • Metro: Buy 72-hour pass (€18) - unlimited metro, bus, tram
  • Walking: Rome is VERY walkable - historic center is compact
  • Dress Code: Shoulders + knees covered for churches (Vatican, too!)
  • Pickpockets: Watch belongings on crowded buses and near tourist sites
  • Water: Free drinking fountains (nasoni) everywhere - refill bottle!
  • Restaurants: Eat where locals eat (away from Colosseum/Trevi)
  • Tipping: Not required in Italy - maybe round up or leave 5-10%

Tomorrow's Preview

Day 3: We head north to Florence! Morning train (90 minutes), check into your hotel with Duomo views, and start exploring the Renaissance capital. Uffizi Gallery, Ponte Vecchio, and your first bistecca fiorentina await!

Note: Book your Uffizi and Accademia tickets for Days 3-4 ASAP - they sell out!
