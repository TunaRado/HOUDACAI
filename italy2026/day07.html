🎨 Your Day, Your Choice

Options: Lake Como day trip, Milan design scene, or relaxed city exploration

Option A: Lake Como Day Trip

9:00 AM - Train to Lake Como

Escape to the stunning Italian Lakes region

  • Train: Milano Centrale → Como S. Giovanni (1 hour, €5-10)
  • Trains run frequently throughout the day
  • Or: Milano Centrale → Varenna (1 hour, €7-12) - different lakeside town
  • Buy tickets at station or Trenitalia app

10:30 AM - Explore Como or Varenna

Charming lakeside towns with stunning mountain views

  • Como: Larger town, funicular to Brunate for views, Duomo cathedral
  • Varenna: Smaller, more intimate, Villa Monastero gardens
  • Take ferry to Bellagio ("Pearl of Lake Como")
  • Lunch at lakeside restaurant
  • Walk the romantic promenades
  • Ferry rides between towns (€5-10 per ride)
  • Return to Milan by 5-6 PM for evening aperitivo

Option B: Milan Design & Culture Day

10:00 AM - Brunch & Coffee

Sleep in and enjoy a leisurely morning

  • 10 Corso Como: Fashion concept store with café and gallery
  • Pavé: Hip bakery in NoLo district
  • Enjoy quality coffee and pastries

11:30 AM - Design District (Zona Tortona/5 Vie)

Milan's reputation as design capital

  • Browse independent design shops and galleries
  • Visit Fondazione Prada - contemporary art museum (€12)
  • Architecture photography opportunities
  • Superstudio - design showroom (if events happening)
  • Discover hidden courtyards and modernist buildings

1:30 PM - Lunch in Isola

Milan's hipster neighborhood

  • Modern, multicultural area with street art
  • See Bosco Verticale (Vertical Forest) buildings - architectural icon
  • Trendy restaurants and cafés
  • Fresco & Fornelli: Creative Italian (€20-35 pp)

3:00 PM - Shopping or Museums

Choose your afternoon adventure

  • Shopping: Corso Buenos Aires (high street brands, less expensive)
  • Art: Pinacoteca di Brera (€15) - Italian Renaissance masterpieces
  • Modern Art: Museo del Novecento (€10) - 20th century Italian art
  • Vintage: Cavalli e Nastri - designer vintage clothing
  • Or simply relax in Sempione Park

Evening (Both Options)

6:00 PM - Final Milan Aperitivo

Make it special for your last night

  • Ceresio 7: Rooftop pools and views (dress up, €15 drinks)
  • Terrazza Aperol: Duomo views (touristy but iconic)
  • Navigli: Return to favorite canal spot
  • Dry Milano: Creative cocktails, speakeasy vibe
  • Toast to three fantastic days in Milan!

8:00 PM - Farewell Dinner

Splurge or keep it casual

  • Splurge Option: Contraste (Michelin star, €100+ pp)
  • Classic Option: Ratanà (modern Milanese, €40-60 pp)
  • Casual Option: Spontini pizza or Luini panzerotti
  • Order anything you haven't tried yet!
  • Don't miss: Ossobuco if you haven't had it

10:00 PM - Last Night in Milan

Soak in the Milanese atmosphere

  • Walk through illuminated Duomo square one last time
  • Final gelato or digestivo
  • Navigli canal-side nightcap
  • Live music if you find a good spot
  • Pack tonight! Train to Florence tomorrow
  • Hotel checkout at 11 AM, train around 12-1 PM

📍 Lake Como or Milan Design Districts

💡 Day 7 Tips

  • Lake Como: Check weather forecast - go if sunny! Cloudy/rainy? Stay in Milan.
  • Como Timing: Leave early, return by 6 PM for Milan aperitivo
  • Ferry Pass: Buy day pass if island-hopping on Como (€15-20)
  • Bellagio: Most beautiful but most touristy - go early
  • Shopping: Milan outlets are outside city - not worth trip on short visit
  • Packing: Tomorrow you head to Florence - pack light clothes for warm Tuscany
  • Train Tickets: Confirm your Milan → Florence tickets for tomorrow

🍕 Milan Food Checklist

Have you tried all of these yet?

  • ☐ Risotto alla Milanese (saffron risotto)
  • ☐ Ossobuco (braised veal shank)
  • ☐ Cotoletta alla Milanese (breaded veal cutlet)
  • ☐ Panzerotti from Luini (fried pocket pizza)
  • ☐ Aperol Spritz during aperitivo
  • ☐ Negroni Sbagliato
  • ☐ Panettone or Milan pastries
  • ☐ Michelin experience (or save budget for Florence/Rome!)

💡 Lake Como Quick Guide

If You Choose Lake Como:

  • Como town: Base for exploration, funicular to Brunate hilltop
  • Bellagio: "Pearl of the lake" - most famous, very pretty
  • Varenna: Quieter, romantic, Villa Monastero gardens
  • Tremezzo: Villa Carlotta with beautiful gardens
  • Ferry tips: Slower boats cheaper, fast boats pricier but save time
  • Lunch: Lakeside restaurants can be pricey - budget €30-50 pp
  • Photos: Lake Como is STUNNING - charge your camera/phone!
