Option A: Day Trip to Tivoli

Villa d'Este & Hadrian's Villa

Escape to the hills - stunning Renaissance gardens and Roman ruins

  • Getting There: Train from Roma Tiburtina to Tivoli (1 hour, €2-4)
  • Or organized tour (€50-70 pp with transport and guide)
  • Villa d'Este: €12, incredible fountains and Renaissance gardens
  • 100+ fountains, water features everywhere
  • Organ Fountain plays music!
  • Terrace views over Roman countryside
  • UNESCO World Heritage site
  • Hadrian's Villa: €10, sprawling 2nd-century imperial complex
  • Massive ruins of Emperor Hadrian's retreat
  • Pools, temples, theaters, libraries
  • Can easily spend 2-3 hours exploring
  • Lunch: Tivoli town has good restaurants (€20-35 pp)
  • Return to Rome late afternoon

Option B: Ancient Rome Extension

Ostia Antica

Better than Pompeii? Many think so!

  • Getting There: Metro B to Piramide, then train to Ostia Antica (30 min, €1.50)
  • Tickets: €12
  • Ancient Roman port city - incredibly well-preserved
  • Much less crowded than Pompeii
  • Mosaics, frescoes, theaters, public baths
  • Ancient apartment buildings still standing
  • Forum, temples, warehouses
  • Allow 3-4 hours to explore
  • Bring picnic or eat at on-site café
  • Return to Rome mid-afternoon

Option C: Rome Revisited

Do it All Again!

Return to favorites or catch what you missed

  • Trevi Fountain at Dawn: Get there 6-7 AM - you'll have it to yourself!
  • Colosseum at Sunset: Different vibe, beautiful light
  • Markets: Campo de' Fiori morning market (fruit, veg, flowers)
  • Porta Portese Flea Market: Sunday mornings only (tomorrow!) - massive
  • Villa Pamphili: Rome's largest park, locals' favorite
  • Appian Way: Ancient Roman road, rent bike and ride
  • Catacombs: San Callisto or San Sebastiano (€10, guided tours)
  • Or simply wander with no map!

Option D: Museum Marathon

More Art & Culture

For the museum lovers

  • Palazzo Doria Pamphilj: Private palace, Velázquez portrait (€14)
  • Palazzo Barberini: Caravaggio, Raphael, Bernini (€12)
  • MAXXI: Modern art museum by Zaha Hadid (€12)
  • Ara Pacis: Augustus's altar of peace (€10.50)
  • Palazzo Massimo: Ancient Roman frescoes and mosaics (€10)
  • Baths of Caracalla: Massive ancient bath complex (€8)
  • Santa Maria Maggiore: Beautiful basilica (free)
