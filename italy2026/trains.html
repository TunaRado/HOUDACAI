Why Train Travel in Italy?

Italian trains are the BEST way to travel between cities. The Frecciarossa high-speed trains are fast (200+ mph!), comfortable, punctual, and drop you right in city centers. No airport security hassles, no baggage fees, no arriving 2 hours early. Just show up 10 minutes before departure, hop on, and enjoy scenic countryside views. It's civilized travel at its finest.

🚉 Train Station Tips

Before You Go

  • Print or download e-tickets to phone
  • Arrive 15-20 minutes early
  • Check departure board for platform (binario)
  • No security or check-in required!
  • Validate regional tickets (not Frecciarossa)

At the Station

  • Find your platform on departure board
  • Look for your car number on platform
  • Board any time before departure
  • Luggage racks at end of each car
  • Snack car mid-train (overpriced but convenient)

On the Train

  • Seat numbers on tickets are reserved
  • Free WiFi (can be slow)
  • Power outlets at most seats
  • Bathrooms at end of cars
  • Enjoy the view - Italian countryside is stunning!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to print my ticket?

No! E-tickets on your phone are fine. Just show the QR code if a conductor checks.

How much luggage can I bring?

No official limit! Bring what you can carry. Overhead racks fit carry-on sized bags, larger bags go in luggage areas at the end of cars.

Is food allowed on the train?

Absolutely! Bring sandwiches, wine, snacks. Many Italians pack picnics for longer journeys. Just clean up after yourself.

What if I miss my train?

Budget tickets are non-refundable and non-changeable. Flexible tickets can be changed (with fee). Arrive early to avoid this!

Should I book 1st or 2nd class?

2nd class is perfectly comfortable for these short trips (all under 3 hours). Save money for gelato! 1st class is nice but not necessary.
