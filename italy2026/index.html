Which Italian Dream Will You Choose?

We've crafted five incredible 2-week adventures through Italy and beyond. Each journey offers something unique - from iconic classics to hidden gems, coastal paradise to alpine peaks. Browse the options below and discover which adventure speaks to your heart!

The Five Adventures

1

🌊 Coastal Paradise

Rome → Florence → Naples → Amalfi → Rome

Southern Italy's greatest hits: Renaissance art, world's best pizza, Pompeii ruins, and the stunning Amalfi Coast. Perfect blend of culture, history, and Mediterranean beauty.

13 nights 4 regions $6,000-7,500

Top Highlights:

  • 🍕 Authentic Neapolitan pizza in its birthplace
  • 🏛️ Pompeii frozen in time
  • 🌅 Path of the Gods coastal hike
  • 🎨 Florence's Renaissance masterpieces
  • ⛪ Vatican & Colosseum in Rome
Pace: Moderate • Best For: Foodies, history lovers, beach enthusiasts
Explore This Adventure →
2

🎭 Classic Italy

Venice → Milan → Florence → Rome

The iconic north-to-south journey everyone dreams of. From floating Venice to fashionable Milan, artistic Florence to ancient Rome - this is Italy's greatest hits tour.

13 nights 4 cities $6,800-8,200

Top Highlights:

  • 🚣 Gondola rides through Venice canals
  • 🎨 Da Vinci's Last Supper in Milan
  • 🗿 Michelangelo's David in Florence
  • 🏛️ Roman Forum & Colosseum
  • ⛪ Sistine Chapel ceiling
Pace: Moderate • Best For: First-timers, art enthusiasts, culture lovers
Explore This Adventure →
3

⛰️ Alpine to Ancient

Zurich → Lucerne → Milan → Florence → Rome

Start in pristine Swiss Alps, then descend through scenic mountain passes into Italy. Experience two countries: Swiss precision and Italian passion in one unforgettable trip.

13 nights 5 cities $7,200-8,800

Top Highlights:

  • 🏔️ Mt. Pilatus or Mt. Titlis from Lucerne
  • 🚂 Scenic train through Swiss Alps to Italy
  • 🧀 Fondue in Switzerland, pasta in Italy
  • 🎭 Milan's Last Supper & fashion district
  • 🏛️ Florence Renaissance + Rome ancient sites
Pace: Active • Best For: Nature lovers, train enthusiasts, two-country seekers
Explore This Adventure →
4

💎 Hidden Gems

Ljubljana → Lake Bled → Venice → Florence → Rome

Discover Slovenia's fairy tale beauty before diving into Italian classics. Best value trip with unique experiences nobody else has - plus all the Italian highlights!

13 nights 5 destinations $6,200-7,800

Top Highlights:

  • 🏰 Lake Bled fairy tale island church
  • 🕳️ Postojna Cave (world-famous caverns)
  • 🏙️ Ljubljana's charming old town (CHEAP!)
  • 🚣 Venice canals & St. Mark's Square
  • 🎨 Florence art + Rome history finale
Pace: Moderate • Best For: Budget travelers, adventure seekers, unique experiences
Explore This Adventure →
5

🍷 Central Italy Intensive

Rome → Tuscany → Umbria → Rome

Deep dive into central Italy's art, wine, and hill towns. Slower pace, deeper experiences. Perfect for wine lovers and those seeking authentic Italy beyond the tourist trail.

13 nights 3 regions $6,500-8,000

Top Highlights:

  • 🍷 Chianti wine country (Greve, Castellina)
  • 🏰 Medieval hill towns (San Gimignano, Siena)
  • ⛪ Assisi's stunning basilica
  • 🎨 Orvieto's underground city
  • 🏛️ Extended Rome exploration (4 nights)
Pace: Relaxed • Best For: Wine lovers, slow travel, hill town enthusiasts
