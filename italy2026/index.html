Essential Information

💶 Currency & Budget

  • Currency: Euro (€)
  • Estimated Exchange: $1 = €0.92 (Dec 2025)
  • Daily Budget: €120-180 per person
  • Total Trip: $5,500-7,200 for two (14 nights)
  • ATMs: Best exchange rates (avoid airport)
  • Credit Cards: Widely accepted, notify bank

📱 Staying Connected

  • Phone: T-Mobile/Verizon international plans
  • Alternative: Italian SIM card (TIM, Vodafone)
  • WiFi: Free at hotels, cafés
  • Apps to Download: Google Maps (offline), Google Translate, Trenitalia
  • Power: Type C/F plugs, 230V

🎫 Booking Essentials

  • Flights: Book NOW (Dec 2025-Feb 2026)
  • Hotels: Book by Feb-March 2026
  • Last Supper: Book 3 months ahead!
  • Vatican: Book 2-3 months ahead
  • Uffizi/Accademia: 2 months ahead
  • Trains: Book 60-90 days out for best prices

🌍 Travel Tips

  • Passport: Must be valid 6 months beyond trip
  • Visa: Not required for US citizens (up to 90 days)
  • Language: English widely spoken in tourist areas
  • Tipping: Not required but appreciated (5-10%)
  • Water: Tap water safe to drink everywhere
  • Churches: Shoulders + knees covered required
