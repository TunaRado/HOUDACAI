🚄 Rome → Florence (Firenze)

Train: Frecciarossa high-speed

Duration: 1 hour 30 minutes

Route: Roma Termini → Firenze S.M.N. (Santa Maria Novella)

Cost: €40-60 per person (book 60-90 days ahead for best price)

Today's Itinerary

8:00 AM - Breakfast

Start your day with a traditional Italian breakfast

  • Hotel breakfast (included) or local café
  • Coffee: Cappuccino before 11 AM only! Then espresso
  • Cornetto (Italian croissant) with jam or cream
  • Recommended: Pasticceria Tonolo - best pastries in Venice

9:00 AM - St. Mark's Basilica

Arrive early to beat the crowds at this stunning Byzantine masterpiece

  • Free entry to the basilica (small fee for special areas)
  • Dress code: Shoulders and knees covered
  • Marvel at the golden mosaics (28,000 sq ft of gold!)
  • Optional: Pala d'Oro (€5) - golden altarpiece with 2,000+ gems
  • Optional: Basilica Museum & Terrace (€7) - see the bronze horses, great views
  • Duration: 60-90 minutes

11:00 AM - Doge's Palace (Palazzo Ducale)

Explore the seat of Venetian power and cross the famous Bridge of Sighs

  • Tickets: €30 (includes Correr Museum) - book online to skip line
  • See the grand chambers and Hall of the Great Council
  • Walk across the Bridge of Sighs
  • Visit the prison cells where Casanova was held
  • Special: Secret Itineraries Tour (€35, book ahead) - hidden passages
  • Duration: 90-120 minutes

1:00 PM - Lunch Break

Cicchetti crawl in the Castello or San Polo districts

  • Osteria Al Portego - Castello, excellent cicchetti selection
  • Osteria Bancogiro - Grand Canal views, creative small plates
  • Order 4-6 cicchetti per person + wine/spritz
  • Budget: €20-30 per person

2:30 PM - Gondola Ride 🛶

The quintessential Venice experience!

  • Official price: €80-100 for 30-40 minutes (up to 6 people)
  • Negotiate the duration and route before boarding
  • Best routes: Small canals, not just Grand Canal
  • Share with another couple to split cost
  • Tip: €5-10 if gondolier is excellent
  • Alternative: Traghetto gondola ferry (€2, standing only, quick cross-canal ride)
  • Recommended start: Near Campo Santa Maria Formosa or Rialto

4:00 PM - Explore Dorsoduro District

Venice's artistic neighborhood with a more local feel

  • Accademia Gallery - Venetian art masterpieces (€15, 60 min)
  • Walk along Zattere waterfront - great for aperitivo
  • Visit Santa Maria della Salute church (stunning architecture)
  • Browse Campo Santa Margherita - student area, lively piazza
  • Stop for gelato at Gelateria Nico (famous gianduiotto)

6:00 PM - Aperitivo Time

Experience the Italian pre-dinner ritual

  • Find a canal-side bacaro or bar
  • Order Aperol Spritz or Prosecco
  • Enjoy free snacks that come with drinks
  • Cantinone Già Schiavi - Dorsoduro, canal-side gem
  • Al Timon - Cannaregio, locals' favorite

8:00 PM - Dinner

Splurge on a memorable Venetian dinner

  • Osteria alle Testiere - Tiny, incredible seafood (book WAY ahead!)
  • Antiche Carampane - Hidden spot, traditional recipes
  • Trattoria Antiche Carampane - Locals' favorite, no tourist menus
  • Start: Antipasto misto di pesce (mixed seafood appetizer)
  • Primi: Spaghetti alle vongole or risotto al nero
  • Secondi: Grilled branzino or mixed fried seafood
  • Wine: Soave or Vermentino (white), Valpolicella (red)
  • Budget: €50-70 per person

10:00 PM - Evening Stroll

Venice by night is incredibly romantic and peaceful

  • Walk through now-quiet St. Mark's Square
  • Cross Rialto Bridge with evening lights
  • Get lost one more time in the magical alleyways
  • Gelato stop: Suso or Alaska Gelateria
  • Watch the moon reflect on the Grand Canal

📍 St. Mark's Square & Rialto Area

💡 Pro Tips for Today

  • Gondola Timing: Late afternoon (4-5 PM) has beautiful light but more traffic. Early morning is peaceful.
  • Save Money: Share a gondola with another couple - it's meant for up to 6 people!
  • St. Mark's: Lines shortest before 9 AM or after 4 PM
  • Reservations: Book Doge's Palace online to skip the line
  • Photography: Golden hour (6-7 PM) is magical for canal photos
  • Water Bottle: Fill up at public fountains - Venice has excellent tap water
  • Lunch Strategy: Avoid anywhere with "tourist menu" or picture menus in 5 languages
  • Cash: Have small bills for cicchetti and cafés

🎯 Optional Activities

If You Have Extra Time or Energy:

  • Scala Contarini del Bovolo: Hidden spiral staircase with rooftop views (€7)
  • Libreria Acqua Alta: Quirky bookshop with books in bathtubs and gondolas
  • Rooftop Views: T Fondaco dei Tedeschi (free, book time slot online)
  • Jewish Ghetto: Historic neighborhood in Cannaregio, excellent kosher bakeries
  • Sunset Cruise: Vaporetto Line 2 down Grand Canal (use your pass!)

🍷 Wine & Drink Guide

  • Aperol Spritz: The Venetian aperitif - Aperol, Prosecco, soda (€6-10)
  • Prosecco: From Veneto region - order by the glass or bottle
  • Ombra: Small glass of wine - traditional Venetian way to drink
  • Soave: Crisp white wine, perfect with seafood
  • Valpolicella: Light red wine from nearby region
  • Amarone: Bold, expensive red - for special occasions
