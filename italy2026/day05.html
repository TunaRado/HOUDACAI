🚄 Venice → Milan

Train: Frecciarossa (high-speed)

Duration: 2 hours 30 minutes

Route: Venice Santa Lucia → Milano Centrale

Cost: €35-55 per person (book 60-90 days ahead)

Today's Itinerary

8:00 AM - Last Venetian Breakfast

Enjoy your final morning in the floating city

  • Hotel breakfast with Grand Canal views
  • Or visit a favorite café one last time
  • Pack and organize luggage
  • Hotel checkout by 11 AM

9:30 AM - Final Venice Morning

A few last hours to soak in the magic

  • Quick walk through favorite spots
  • Last-minute souvenir shopping
  • Coffee at a canal-side café
  • Take final photos

11:15 AM - To the Train Station

Venice Santa Lucia station

  • Walk or vaporetto to Venezia S. Lucia station
  • Arrive 30 minutes before departure
  • Validate ticket at yellow machines (if paper ticket)
  • Find platform (binario) - usually announced 20 min before
  • Grab snacks/water for the journey

12:00 PM - Frecciarossa Departure

The Italian high-speed train experience

  • Board the sleek Frecciarossa train
  • Find your reserved seat (car and seat number on ticket)
  • Stow luggage in overhead racks or at car ends
  • Relax in comfortable, modern train
  • Watch Italian countryside fly by at 180+ mph
  • Free WiFi available on board
  • Café car for snacks and drinks

2:30 PM - Arrive Milano Centrale

Welcome to Milan - Italy's economic powerhouse!

  • Exit train and navigate impressive station (architectural masterpiece)
  • Follow M2 (green line) or M3 (yellow line) metro signs
  • Purchase metro tickets (€2 single, €7 for 24-hour pass)
  • Take M3 to Duomo station (2 stops, 5 minutes)

3:00 PM - Hotel Check-In

Room Mate Giulia - 2 minutes walk from Duomo!

  • Modern design hotel with rooftop terrace
  • Address: Silvio Pellico 4, Duomo area
  • Drop bags and freshen up
  • Get hotel recommendations and map

4:00 PM - First Glimpse of the Duomo

Milan's cathedral - one of the largest in the world!

  • Walk to Piazza del Duomo (literally 2 minutes!)
  • Marvel at the Gothic masterpiece exterior
  • Take photos from different angles in the plaza
  • See Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II (luxury shopping arcade)
  • Window shop at Prada, Louis Vuitton, etc.
  • Pro tip: Save Duomo interior/rooftop for tomorrow

5:30 PM - Explore Brera District

Milan's artistic neighborhood

  • Walk to Brera (15-minute stroll from Duomo)
  • Charming cobblestone streets and art galleries
  • Browse boutiques and artisan shops
  • Visit Pinacoteca di Brera (art museum) if time/interest
  • Relax in small piazzas

7:00 PM - Aperitivo Milanese-Style

Milan invented the aperitivo buffet!

  • How it works: Buy a drink (€8-12), get free buffet access!
  • Recommended spots:
  • Ceresio 7 - Rooftop with amazing views (dress nicely)
  • Terrazza Aperol - Duomo views, Aperol Spritz birthplace
  • Camparino in Galleria - Historic, elegant
  • 10 Corso Como - Fashion-forward crowd
  • Eat enough appetizers to make a light dinner!

9:00 PM - Dinner or Light Bite

Depending on how much aperitivo you had!

  • Full Dinner Option: Trattoria Milanese (traditional risotto)
  • Pizza Option: Spontini (Milan-style thick pizza)
  • Light Option: Gelato and evening stroll
  • Walk around illuminated Duomo
  • Explore Corso Vittorio Emanuele (shopping street)

10:30 PM - Evening Wind-Down

First night in Milan

  • Return to hotel
  • Enjoy rooftop terrace if weather is nice
  • Plan tomorrow's Last Supper visit (MUST BOOK IN ADVANCE!)
  • Confirm your Last Supper ticket time

📍 Milan City Center

💡 Milan Arrival Tips

  • Train Tickets: Book Trenitalia or Italo online in advance for best prices
  • Luggage: Wheels handle well on Italian trains - not many stairs
  • Milano Centrale: Beautiful fascist-era architecture - take a photo!
  • Metro: Clean, efficient, easy. Get 24-48 hour pass if using multiple times
  • Last Supper: BOOK NOW if you haven't! Tickets sell out weeks ahead
  • Milan Style: Milanese dress well. Step up your fashion game!
  • Dining Hours: Dinner starts 8 PM earliest (aperitivo 6-8 PM)
  • Hotel Location: You're 2 min from Duomo - perfect central base!

🍝 Milan vs Venice Food

What's Different in Milan

  • Risotto alla Milanese: Saffron risotto - Milan's signature dish
  • Ossobuco: Braised veal shank - traditional with risotto
  • Cotoletta alla Milanese: Breaded veal cutlet (like schnitzel)
  • Panettone: Milan's Christmas sweet bread (year-round now!)
  • Aperitivo Culture: Way bigger in Milan than Venice
  • Less Seafood: More meat-focused inland cuisine

⚠️ Important: Last Supper Tickets!

Book NOW if you haven't already!

Il Cenacolo (The Last Supper) by Leonardo da Vinci

  • Location: Santa Maria delle Grazie church
  • Official site: cenacolovinciano.org
  • Cost: €15 + €2 booking fee
  • Timeslots: Every 15 minutes, only 25 people per slot
  • Booking window: Opens ~3 months in advance
  • Sells out FAST! If official site sold out, try Viator/GetYourGuide (pricier but available)
  • Plan tomorrow's schedule around your Last Supper time!
