✈️ Farewell to the Eternal City

Departure Day Timeline

7:00 AM - Wake Up

Last morning in Italy

  • Set alarm with buffer time
  • Final check: passport, tickets, valuables
  • Double-check hotel room for forgotten items
  • Charge devices for flight

8:00 AM - Final Italian Breakfast

Savor every last bite

  • Hotel breakfast one last time
  • Or visit nearby café for final cappuccino and cornetto
  • Take your time - soak in the Italian breakfast vibe
  • Remember: After 11 AM it's espresso only, not cappuccino!

9:00 AM - Last Roman Morning

Final moments in the city

  • Quick walk to nearby sights one last time
  • Maybe Trevi Fountain for final coin toss
  • Or just wander streets near hotel
  • Take final photos
  • Buy any last-minute items
  • Duration: 60-90 minutes max

10:30 AM - Hotel Checkout

Prepare for departure

  • Return to hotel
  • Final room check for belongings
  • Checkout at front desk (usually 11 AM deadline)
  • Tip cleaning staff (€2-5 per night total)
  • Get receipt and confirm all charges

11:00 AM - To the Airport

Multiple options for Fiumicino Airport (FCO)

  • Leonardo Express Train (RECOMMENDED):
  • From Roma Termini to FCO Airport (32 min, €14 pp)
  • Departs every 15-30 minutes
  • Non-stop, direct service
  • Most reliable option
  • Taxi/Uber:
  • Fixed rate €50 from city center to FCO
  • 45-60 minutes depending on traffic
  • Door-to-door convenience
  • Good if you have lots of luggage
  • Private Transfer:
  • Book ahead (€50-70)
  • Driver meets you at hotel
  • Arrive 3 hours before international flight!

12:00 PM - Arrive at Fiumicino Airport

Rome's main international airport

  • Follow signs to your airline terminal
  • Terminal 1: Budget airlines
  • Terminal 3: Major airlines (Delta, American, United, etc.)
  • Check-In: Use kiosk or desk
  • Drop checked bags
  • Get boarding pass

12:30 PM - Security & Customs

Through the checkpoints

  • Passport control - may have lines
  • Security screening
  • Remember: liquids 3.4oz/100ml max in clear bag
  • Wine/olive oil must be in checked bags!

1:00 PM - Duty-Free & Airport Lounge

Post-security waiting area

  • Last-minute shopping:
  • Duty-free alcohol, perfume, chocolate
  • Italian specialty foods (sealed packages OK for customs)
  • Designer goods at duty-free prices
  • Lunch Options:
  • Various cafés and restaurants in terminal
  • Last Italian meal - pizza or pasta!
  • Pricey but decent quality for airport
  • Airport Lounges:
  • If you have lounge access: free food, drinks, WiFi, showers
  • Priority Pass accepted at several lounges
  • Pay-per-use: €30-40 (sometimes worth it for comfort)

2:30 PM - Board Flight

Time to say goodbye to Italy

  • Find your gate (check monitors for updates)
  • Gates can be far - allow 10-15 min walk
  • Boarding usually 30-45 min before departure
  • Find your seat and settle in
  • Stow luggage carefully

3:30 PM - Takeoff

Watch Italy disappear beneath you

  • Look out the window one last time
  • See the Italian coastline as you depart
  • Flight time: ~11-12 hours to Tampa (with any connections)
  • Usually 1 stop (via Frankfurt, Munich, NYC, etc.)
  • In-flight:
  • Watch movies you missed
  • Organize and edit photos
  • Write in travel journal
  • Start planning your return trip! 😊
  • Sleep if you can

~6:30 PM - Land in Tampa

Welcome home! (Eastern time, same day due to time change)

  • Clear US Customs and Immigration
  • Collect checked bags
  • Agriculture check (declare any food items!)
  • Exit to arrivals area
  • Pickup, Uber, or car service home
  • Unpack, shower, sleep in your own bed
  • Jet lag will hit - be gentle with yourself!

Post-Trip Recovery

Sunday, July 5 - Recovery Day

Built-in rest day before work!

  • Sleep in or wake early (jet lag lottery!)
  • Unpack fully - don't leave bags sitting
  • Do laundry
  • Go through photos and start organizing
  • Grocery shopping - replenish fridge
  • Try to stay awake until 9-10 PM local time (helps reset clock)
  • Light exercise if you feel up to it
  • Start meal-prepping for the week
  • Share favorite photos with friends/family

Monday, July 6 - Back to Work

Re-entry to real life

  • Set multiple alarms (jet lag is real!)
  • Bring Italian treats to share with coworkers
  • Ease back into routine
  • Coffee will taste different (American coffee after Italian espresso... rough!)
  • Start planning your next trip! 😉

💡 Travel Day Tips

  • 3-Hour Rule: Arrive 3 hours before international flights - FCO can have long lines
  • Leonardo Express: Most reliable airport transport - avoid traffic
  • Liquids: Wine/oil must be checked! Don't try to carry on
  • VAT Refund: If you spent €155+, get tax-free forms stamped at airport before check-in
  • Medication: Keep in carry-on with prescription labels
  • Valuables: Keep in carry-on (camera, laptop, jewelry)
  • Download Entertainment: Save movies/shows before flight for offline viewing
  • Hydrate: Drink lots of water on plane - helps with jet lag
  • Move Around: Get up and walk on long flight - prevent DVT
  • Set Watch Back: Change to EST during flight to start adjusting

✈️ Customs & Declarations

What You Can Bring Back to the USA

  • Duty-Free Allowance: $800 per person
  • Alcohol: 1 liter duty-free (21+ only)
  • Additional bottles: ~$1-2 duty per bottle (usually)
  • Food: Most packaged food OK, declare everything!
  • NO: Fresh fruits, vegetables, meats, cheeses with mold
  • YES: Sealed cheeses, dried pasta, olive oil, balsamic, chocolate
  • Truffle products: Usually OK if commercially packaged
  • Be honest: Declare everything - penalties are harsh for lying!

🎒 Packing Tips for Flight Home

  • Carry-On: Valuables, medications, one change of clothes
  • Check Wine Carefully: Bubble wrap + center of bag surrounded by clothes
  • Weight Limits: Usually 50 lbs checked, 22 lbs carry-on (check your airline!)
  • Souvenirs: Pack fragile items with clothes for padding
  • Dirty Laundry: Use plastic bags to separate from clean clothes
  • Documents: Keep passport, tickets, hotel receipts in carry-on
  • Electronics: Chargers, adapters, power banks in carry-on
  • Neck Pillow: Makes long flight much more comfortable
