🍷 Tuscany - Wine Country Paradise

4 Nights • Days 4-7

Arrive Monday, depart Friday

Where You'll Stay:

Option A: Florence base - Hotel Brunelleschi, day trips to wine country (~$240/night)

Option B: Siena base - Hotel in Chianti region, more authentic (~$180-220/night)

Recommendation: Split it! 2 nights Florence + 2 nights Siena area

What You'll Do:

  • Florence highlights - David, Uffizi, Duomo climb
  • Chianti wine tour - Greve, Castellina, vineyard tastings
  • Siena old town - Piazza del Campo, medieval beauty
  • San Gimignano - Medieval towers, gelato world champion!
  • Bistecca Fiorentina feast - The legendary T-bone
  • Pienza & Montepulciano - Val d'Orcia hill towns
  • Truffle hunting (seasonal) - Or just truffle pasta!

Wine Experiences:

  • Castello di Brolio - Historic wine estate
  • Antinori nel Chianti Classico - Modern winery, stunning
  • Fonterutoli - Family-run, authentic
  • Brunello tastings in Montalcino

Where to Eat:

  • Trattoria ZàZà (Florence) - Bistecca heaven
  • Osteria Le Logge (Siena) - Local specialties
  • La Bottega del 30 (Siena) - Tasting menu
  • Any agriturismo - Farm-to-table perfection

⛪ Umbria - Italy's Green Heart

3 Nights • Days 8-10

Arrive Friday, depart Monday

Where You'll Stay:

Option A: Perugia base - University town energy, central for day trips (~$180/night)

Option B: Assisi - Hilltop monastery town, spiritual & beautiful (~$160-200/night)

What You'll Do:

  • Assisi Basilica - St. Francis, stunning frescoes
  • Perugia old town - Medieval core, Perugina chocolate factory
  • Orvieto - Cathedral, underground city, white wine!
  • Spoleto - Roman theater, medieval aqueduct
  • Lake Trasimeno - Peaceful lakeside escape
  • Truffle dishes - Umbria is truffle capital!
  • Sagrantino wine tasting - Montefalco specialty

Why Umbria?

Called "the green heart of Italy" - Tuscany's quieter, cheaper cousin. Same beauty, fewer tourists, more authentic experiences. This is where Italians vacation!

Where to Eat:

  • Trattoria Pallotta (Assisi) - Local Umbrian cuisine
  • La Taverna (Perugia) - Traditional truffles
  • Trattoria del Moro Aronne (Orvieto) - Wild boar pasta

Should You Rent a Car?

🚄 All Trains

Pros:

  • No driving stress or parking hassles
  • Drink wine freely! No designated driver
  • Cheaper overall (~$250-350 vs $450+)
  • Relax and enjoy scenery

Cons:

  • Less flexibility for small hill towns
  • Tied to train schedules
  • Some wineries harder to reach

🚗 Rent Car (Tuscany/Umbria only)

Pros:

  • Total freedom to explore
  • Stop at any winery or viewpoint
  • Reach tiny hill towns easily
  • Romantic countryside drives
  • Grocery store runs for picnics

Cons:

  • More expensive (~$450 total)
  • Parking can be tricky in old towns
  • One person can't drink at tastings
  • ZTL zones (restricted areas) to navigate

Our Recommendation: Rent car for 4-5 days (Florence through Umbria). Train for Rome portions. Best of both!
