Switzerland Travel Tips

💰 Cost Awareness

Switzerland is EXPENSIVE. Budget dinner: €50-70 per person. Coffee: €5-7. But it's worth it!

🚂 Swiss Travel Pass

Consider 6-day pass (~$300 pp) for unlimited trains, boats, mountain railways. Usually saves money!

🏔️ Weather Check

Mountain weather changes fast. Bring layers even in summer. Check Mt. Pilatus webcams before going up!

💳 Payment

Swiss Franc (CHF), NOT Euro (though some places accept Euro). Credit cards everywhere. Tap-to-pay standard.
