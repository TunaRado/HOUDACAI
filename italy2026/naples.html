Welcome to Naples - The Soul of Southern Italy

Naples is raw, authentic, and utterly captivating. This is where pizza was born, where Vesuvius looms dramatically over the bay, where ancient Pompeii lies frozen in time, and where Italian street life reaches its most vibrant crescendo. Gritty yet beautiful, chaotic yet charming, Naples will steal your heart with its passion, history, and unparalleled food scene.

🗺️ Must-Do Naples Experiences

🏛️ Pompeii Day Trip

Essential Experience!

Walk through streets frozen in time since 79 AD. This ancient Roman city, buried by Vesuvius, is incredibly well-preserved. See homes, temples, theaters, and even street food stalls.

Time: Half day (4-5 hours)

Getting There: 40-min train from Naples to Pompei Scavi

Cost: €18 entry + guided tour recommended

🍕 Pizza Pilgrimage

The Birthplace of Pizza!

Naples invented pizza, and nobody does it better. L'Antica Pizzeria da Michele (as seen in "Eat Pray Love"), Di Matteo, and Sorbillo are legendary spots.

Time: Lunch or dinner (expect lines!)

Must Try: Margherita & Marinara

Cost: €5-12 per pizza (incredibly affordable)

🎨 Naples National Archaeological Museum

World-Class Collection

Houses the world's best collection of Greco-Roman artifacts, including treasures from Pompeii and Herculaneum. The mosaics are stunning!

Time: 2-3 hours

Cost: €18

Tip: Book tickets online to skip lines

🏰 Castel dell'Ovo & Waterfront

Seaside Castle

Naples' oldest castle on a small island with stunning bay views. Walk the Via Caracciolo waterfront promenade at sunset.

Time: 1-2 hours

Cost: Free entry

Vibe: Perfect sunset spot

🚇 Naples Metro Art Stations

Subway as Museum

Naples metro stations feature incredible contemporary art installations. Toledo station (designed by Oscar Tusquets) is breathtaking!

Time: 30 minutes - 1 hour

Cost: Just metro fare (€1.10)

Stations: Toledo, Università, Municipio

⛪ Naples Duomo & San Severo Chapel

Religious Art & Mystery

The Duomo houses San Gennaro's blood miracle. Cappella Sansevero features the stunning "Veiled Christ" sculpture - prepare to be amazed.

Time: 1-2 hours

Cost: Duomo free, San Severo €8

Must See: The Veiled Christ sculpture

🍽️ Where to Eat in Naples

L'Antica Pizzeria da Michele

Pizza

The most famous pizzeria in Naples. Only serves Margherita and Marinara, but they're perfection. Expect long lines!

Must Try: Margherita pizza

Price: €5-7

Pizzeria Sorbillo

Pizza

Family-run since 1935, incredible crust, always packed with locals and tourists. More variety than Da Michele.

Must Try: Sorbillo pizza with buffalo mozzarella

Price: €6-10

Pizzeria Di Matteo

Pizza

Where Bill Clinton ate when visiting Naples. Fantastic fried pizza (pizza fritta) - uniquely Neapolitan!

Must Try: Pizza fritta

Price: €5-8

Trattoria da Nennella

Traditional

Chaotic, loud, authentic family-style dining. Shared tables, singing waiters, amazing traditional Neapolitan food.

Must Try: Pasta e patate, ragù napoletano

Price: €15-25

Sfogliatella Mary

The best sfogliatella (flaky pastry with ricotta) in Naples. Grab one warm in the morning!

Must Try: Sfogliatella riccia (warm!)

Cafe

Historic cafe near Piazza Plebiscito. Perfect for espresso and people-watching in elegant surroundings.

💡 Naples Insider Tips

  • Safety: Naples has a reputation, but it's mostly safe. Watch for pickpockets in crowded areas, keep valuables close.
  • Traffic: Naples traffic is legendary chaos. Don't even think about driving - use metro, walk, or taxis.
  • Spaccanapoli: This straight-as-an-arrow street cuts through the old town - perfect for wandering and getting lost.
  • Coffee Culture: Espresso should be drunk standing at the bar (cheaper than sitting). It's a quick ritual.
  • Language: Less English spoken here than Rome/Florence. Learn basic Italian phrases - locals appreciate the effort!
  • Laundry Day: Look up! Neapolitans hang laundry between buildings - it's iconic and charming.
  • Pizza Rules: Don't ask for pineapple. Seriously. Also, eat pizza with a fork and knife (Neapolitan style).
  • Vesuvius View: Best views from waterfront (Via Caracciolo) or hotel rooftops at sunset.
