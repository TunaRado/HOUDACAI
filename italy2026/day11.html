🚄 Florence → Rome

Train: Frecciarossa high-speed

Duration: 1 hour 30 minutes

Route: Firenze S.M.N. → Roma Termini

Cost: €40-60 per person

Rome Stay: 6 nights at Hotel Artemide

Today's Itinerary

9:00 AM - Final Florentine Breakfast

Last morning in Florence

  • Hotel breakfast or favorite café
  • Pack up and hotel checkout by 11 AM
  • Final views from your Duomo-facing room

11:00 AM - To Florence Train Station

Walk to Santa Maria Novella station

  • 15-20 minute walk from most hotels
  • Arrive 30 minutes before departure
  • Validate ticket if paper (not needed for e-tickets)
  • Find platform (binario) on boards

12:30 PM - Train to Rome

Fast journey through Umbria and Lazio

  • Board Frecciarossa train
  • Watch Tuscan hills give way to Roman countryside
  • Pass through Orvieto region
  • Lunch on train or wait for Rome
  • Arrive Roma Termini around 2 PM

2:00 PM - Arrive Rome Termini

Welcome to the Eternal City!

  • Exit Italy's busiest train station
  • Watch for pickpockets - very crowded area
  • Hotel is walking distance from Termini

2:30 PM - Hotel Check-In

Hotel Artemide - 4-star near Termini with rooftop bar!

  • Via Nazionale 22 (5-minute walk from station)
  • Modern boutique hotel with excellent service
  • Request room away from Via Nazionale (street noise)
  • Check out the rooftop bar
  • Get metro map and area recommendations
  • Rest for 30-45 minutes

4:00 PM - First Roman Walk

Explore the historic center

  • Walk or take Metro A to Spagna
  • Spanish Steps: Iconic staircase (recently restored)
  • Via Condotti - luxury shopping street
  • Walk to Trevi Fountain (10 min)
  • Throw coin over left shoulder into fountain (ensures return to Rome!)
  • Battle the crowds but worth it - baroque masterpiece
  • Duration: 60-90 minutes

5:30 PM - Pantheon

2,000-year-old architectural wonder

  • 10-minute walk from Trevi Fountain
  • Free entry! (Recently made free again)
  • Marvel at the oculus (open dome ceiling)
  • Perfect proportions - dome diameter = height
  • Raphael's tomb inside
  • Stand in the center and look up - incredible!
  • Duration: 30 minutes

6:30 PM - Piazza Navona

Rome's beautiful baroque square

  • 2-minute walk from Pantheon
  • Built on ancient Roman stadium foundations
  • Three fountains: Bernini's Fountain of Four Rivers (centerpiece)
  • Street artists, cafés, performers
  • Perfect for aperitivo people-watching
  • Sit and soak in the atmosphere

7:30 PM - Walk to Trastevere

Cross Tiber River to Rome's bohemian heart

  • 20-minute walk from Piazza Navona
  • Cross Ponte Sisto pedestrian bridge
  • Enter Trastevere - cobblestone streets and ivy-covered buildings
  • Explore narrow medieval lanes
  • Super authentic Roman neighborhood

8:00 PM - Dinner in Trastevere

First taste of Roman cuisine

  • Da Enzo al 29: Tiny, incredible, BOOK AHEAD! (€30-45 pp)
  • Tonnarello: No reservations, expect to wait, worth it (€25-40 pp)
  • Flavio al Velavevodetto: Testaccio area, locals' favorite (€25-35 pp)
  • Must try Roman classics:
  • Cacio e pepe (pecorino cheese and black pepper pasta)
  • Carbonara (egg, guanciale, pecorino - NO cream!)
  • Amatriciana (tomato, guanciale, pecorino)
  • Gricia (cacio e pepe + guanciale)
  • Carciofi alla giudia (Jewish-style fried artichokes - in season!)
  • Wine: Frascati (white) or Castelli Romani

10:00 PM - Trastevere Nightlife

Experience Roman evening culture

  • Walk through lively Piazza di Santa Maria in Trastevere
  • Street musicians and performers
  • Gelato from Fior di Luna or Gelateria alla Scala
  • Bar hop on Via del Moro or Via Benedetta
  • Or walk back via Campo de' Fiori
  • Taxi or night bus back to hotel (Trastevere is safe but worth taxi after midnight)

📍 Rome City Center

💡 Rome Arrival Tips

  • Termini Station: Watch for pickpockets! Keep bags close, zippers closed
  • Metro: Buy Roma Pass or 3-day transit pass (€18) if using metro often
  • Walking: Rome is VERY walkable - wear comfortable shoes!
  • Trevi Fountain: VERY crowded - early morning or evening is best
  • Trastevere Dining: Book Da Enzo weeks ahead or arrive at 7 PM opening
  • Carbonara: Authentic Roman has NO cream! Just egg, guanciale, pecorino
  • Drinking Water: Nasoni fountains everywhere - free, cold, safe to drink
  • Dress Code: Churches require covered shoulders/knees - bring shawl/scarf
  • Planning: Book Colosseum underground, Vatican tickets NOW if you haven't!

🍝 Roman Food 101

Rome's cuisine is different from Northern Italy:

  • The Four Pastas: Carbonara, Cacio e Pepe, Amatriciana, Gricia
  • Guanciale: Cured pork jowl - key ingredient (not bacon!)
  • Pecorino Romano: Sharp sheep's milk cheese
  • Carciofi: Artichokes - Roman specialty (spring season)
  • Suppli: Fried rice balls with mozzarella center
  • Saltimbocca: Veal with prosciutto and sage
  • Coda alla vaccinara: Oxtail stew (traditional Roman)
  • Maritozzo: Sweet bun filled with whipped cream (breakfast treat)

🗺️ Rome Neighborhoods Quick Guide

  • Termini: Your hotel area - transport hub, functional not charming
  • Centro Storico: Historic center - Pantheon, Piazza Navona, Trevi
  • Trastevere: Bohemian, authentic, great food and nightlife
  • Monti: Hipster area near Colosseum, vintage shops, cocktail bars
  • Testaccio: Working-class foodie area, authentic trattorias
  • Prati: Near Vatican, upscale residential
  • Jewish Ghetto: Historic neighborhood, excellent restaurants
