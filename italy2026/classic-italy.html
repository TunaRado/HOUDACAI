Your Italian Cities

🚣 Venice - The Floating City

2 Nights • Days 2-3

Arrive Saturday morning, depart Tuesday

Where You'll Stay:

Hotel Antiche Figure - Grand Canal views, near Rialto Bridge (~$260/night)

What You'll Do:

  • Gondola ride at sunset through quiet canals
  • St. Mark's Basilica & Square - Byzantine mosaics, Doge's Palace
  • Murano & Burano islands - Glass blowing, colorful houses
  • Cicchetti crawl - Venetian tapas & spritz hopping
  • Rialto Market - Fresh seafood, local life
  • Get lost in Venice - Best way to explore!

Where to Eat:

  • Osteria alle Testiere - Tiny, amazing seafood
  • Cantina Do Spade - Classic cicchetti
  • Trattoria al Gazzettino - Local favorite, great pasta

👗 Milan - Fashion & Culture Capital

3 Nights • Days 4-6

Arrive Tuesday, depart Friday

Where You'll Stay:

Room Mate Giulia - 2 minutes from Duomo, stylish design (~$220/night)

What You'll Do:

  • Duomo rooftop walk - Walk among Gothic spires, 360° views
  • Last Supper - Book 3 months ahead! Da Vinci's masterpiece
  • Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II - Luxury shopping arcade
  • Navigli district - Canal-side aperitivo & nightlife
  • Sforzesco Castle - Michelangelo's final sculpture
  • Brera district - Art galleries, boutique shops

Where to Eat:

  • Luini - Famous panzerotti (fried pizza pockets)
  • Trattoria Milanese - Traditional risotto alla milanese
  • Pavé - Best pastries for breakfast

🎨 Florence - Renaissance Masterpiece

3 Nights • Days 7-9

Arrive Friday, depart Monday

Where You'll Stay:

Hotel Brunelleschi - Tower with Duomo views, near Piazza della Signoria (~$240/night)

What You'll Do:

  • Accademia Gallery - Michelangelo's David (book ahead!)
  • Uffizi Gallery - Botticelli's Birth of Venus, Renaissance art
  • Duomo climb - 463 steps to top of dome, stunning views
  • Bistecca Fiorentina dinner - Giant T-bone steak!
  • Chianti wine tour - Day trip to wine country
  • Ponte Vecchio - Medieval bridge, gold shops
  • Piazzale Michelangelo - Sunset over Florence

Where to Eat:

  • Trattoria ZàZà - Famous Florentine steak
  • All'Antico Vinaio - Best sandwiches in Italy
  • Gelateria dei Neri - Amazing gelato

🏛️ Rome - The Eternal City

4 Nights • Days 10-13

Arrive Monday, depart Friday for home

Where You'll Stay:

Hotel Artemide - Rooftop bar with views, near Termini station (~$250/night)

What You'll Do:

  • Colosseum Underground - Arena floor & underground tunnels (book ahead!)
  • Vatican Museums - Sistine Chapel, Raphael Rooms (go early!)
  • St. Peter's Basilica - Climb the dome for views
  • Roman Forum & Palatine Hill - Ancient Rome walk
  • Borghese Gallery - Bernini sculptures, Caravaggio paintings
  • Trastevere food crawl - Best neighborhood for dinner
  • Trevi Fountain at night - Make a wish, throw a coin
  • Pantheon - 2,000-year-old architectural marvel
  • Free afternoon - Wander, gelato, shop, relax!

Where to Eat:

  • Flavio al Velavevodetto - Carbonara heaven in Testaccio
  • Pizzarium - Best pizza al taglio (pizza by the slice)
  • Roscioli - Wine bar with incredible pasta
  • Supplì Roma - Try Rome's famous supplì (fried rice balls)
