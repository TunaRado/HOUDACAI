Why Florence?

Florence is where the Renaissance was born. This compact, walkable city cradles more artistic treasures per square mile than anywhere on Earth. Michelangelo's David, Brunelleschi's dome, Botticelli's Birth of Venus, da Vinci's genius - they all call Florence home. Beyond the art, there's the food (bistecca fiorentina!), the wine (Chianti country!), and the lifestyle (gelato twice a day is mandatory). This is Italy at its most cultured, most beautiful, most delicious.

🗺️ Things to Do in Florence

🗿 Accademia Gallery - David

Home of Michelangelo's David. Standing before this 17-foot marble masterpiece is genuinely moving - the detail, the power, the emotion. Worth every penny and every minute in line. Book timed entry weeks ahead!

Cost: €12-16
Hours: Tue-Sun 8:15 AM - 6:50 PM
Book: 2-3 months ahead for summer visits
Duration: 1-1.5 hours
Link: galleriaaccademiafirenze.it
Pro Tip: First entry slot (8:15 AM) = smallest crowds

🎨 Uffizi Gallery

One of the world's greatest art museums. Botticelli's Birth of Venus and Primavera, Caravaggio, da Vinci, Raphael - this is the Renaissance's greatest hits album. Plan 3+ hours minimum. Absolutely unmissable.

Cost: €20-25
Hours: Tue-Sun 8:15 AM - 6:50 PM
Book: MUST book timed entry in advance
Duration: 3-4 hours
Link: uffizi.it
Skip Line: Firenze Card or pre-booked tickets mandatory

⛪ Duomo Complex & Dome Climb

Brunelleschi's dome is an engineering miracle. Climbing the 463 steps between the inner and outer shell is an experience - tight spiral staircases, Vasari frescoes up close, and knockout rooftop views. Book the dome climb separately!

Cost: €18 combo ticket (cathedral free, dome €18)
Dome Hours: Mon-Fri 8:30 AM - 7 PM, Sat 8:30 AM - 5 PM
Book Dome: Online in advance (sells out!)
Duration: 2-3 hours for full complex
Link: duomo.firenze.it
Includes: Cathedral, dome, baptistery, bell tower, museum

🍷 Chianti Wine Country Tour

Escape the city for rolling Tuscan hills, medieval villages, and world-class wine. Visit wineries, taste Chianti Classico, eat farm-to-table lunch. Many tours include San Gimignano or Siena. Worth a full day!

Cost: €50-90 for small group tours
Duration: Full day (9 AM - 6 PM)
Includes: 2-3 wineries, lunch, hilltop towns
Book: GetYourGuide, Viator, or local tour companies
Departs: From Florence city center
Pro Tip: Small group tours (max 8 people) are best

🌉 Ponte Vecchio & Oltrarno

The iconic medieval bridge lined with jewelry shops. Cross it to the Oltrarno (other side of Arno) - the authentic, artisan neighborhood. Leather workshops, antique shops, fewer tourists. This is real Florence.

Cost: Free
Best Time: Sunset for golden light
What to Do: Browse artisan workshops, visit Palazzo Pitti
Shopping: Leather goods, antiques, artisan crafts
Photo Spot: View bridge from Ponte Santa Trinita

🏛️ Palazzo Pitti & Boboli Gardens

Massive Renaissance palace with multiple museums and stunning Boboli Gardens out back. The Palatine Gallery has Raphael's masterpieces. The gardens offer shade, views, and a break from museum crowds.

Cost: €16 palace + gardens combo
Hours: Tue-Sun 8:15 AM - 6:50 PM
Duration: 2-3 hours
Link: uffizi.it/palazzo-pitti
Perfect For: Afternoon after morning museum visit

🥩 Mercato Centrale Food Hall

Two-floor food paradise. Ground floor is the historic market (fresh produce, meat, cheese). Upstairs is the modern food court with amazing vendors. Perfect for lunch or dinner on a budget with variety.

Cost: €8-15 per person for full meal
Hours: Ground floor 7 AM - 2 PM, upstairs 10 AM - midnight
Must-Try: Lampredotto (tripe sandwich), fresh pasta, gelato
Location: Near San Lorenzo market
Vibe: Casual, lively, authentic

🌄 Piazzale Michelangelo Sunset

THE panoramic viewpoint over Florence. At sunset, the city turns golden and the Duomo glows. Bring wine, cheese, and good company. Most romantic spot in Florence. Take the stairs up for exercise or bus #12 if lazy.

Cost: Free
Best Time: 1 hour before sunset
Getting There: 20-min uphill walk or bus #12
Bring: Wine, snacks, camera
Pro Tip: Arrive early to claim a good spot on the wall

⛪ Basilica di Santa Croce

Final resting place of Michelangelo, Galileo, Machiavelli, and Rossini. Stunning frescoes, peaceful cloisters, and fascinating history. Less crowded than the Duomo but equally beautiful.

Cost: €8
Hours: Mon-Sat 9:30 AM - 5:30 PM, Sun 2-5:30 PM
Duration: 1 hour
Dress Code: Covered shoulders and knees
Nearby: Leather school in the cloisters

🍝 Where to Eat in Florence

Trattoria Mario

Traditional Florentine • San Lorenzo

Tiny, no-frills trattoria serving honest Tuscan food since 1953. Shared tables, paper placemats, incredible bistecca. Cash only. Arrive early or prepare to wait. Worth every minute.

Must-Order: Bistecca alla fiorentina (€40-50 for 2 to share), Ribollita soup (€8), Pappardelle al cinghiale (€12)
Price Range: €€ (€20-30 per person)
Hours: Mon-Sat 12-3:30 PM only (closed evenings!)
Location: Via Rosina 2r
Cash Only! ATM nearby

All'Antico Vinaio

Sandwich Shop • Centro

Florence's most famous sandwiches. There's always a line, but it moves fast. MASSIVE sandwiches stuffed with quality meats, cheeses, and vegetables. €5-8 and you're stuffed. Grab and eat by the Arno.

Best Sandwiches: La Favolosa (€7), La Paradiso (€6.50), Create your own (€5-8)
Price Range: € (€5-10 for filling meal)
Hours: 10:30 AM - 10 PM daily
Tip: Order at counter, take to-go, eat by Ponte Vecchio
Warning: Expect lines (but they're fast!)

Gustapizza

Pizza • Oltrarno

Wood-fired Neapolitan-style pizza in a tiny space. Only a few tables, mostly takeaway. Simple, perfect pizzas with quality ingredients. €7-10 for a whole pizza. Cash only.

Pizza Picks: Margherita (€6.50), Bufala e pachino (€9), Salsiccia (€8)
Price Range: € (€7-12 per person)
Location: Via Maggio 46r (Oltrarno)
Hours: 11:30 AM - 3 PM, 7-11 PM
Cash Only! Come prepared

Trattoria ZaZa

Tuscan Classics • San Lorenzo

Tourist-friendly but still great quality. Beautiful outdoor courtyard. Perfect for your first night when you want classic Tuscan dishes without hunting for hidden gems. Reservations accepted.

Highlights: Pappardelle al cinghiale (€14), Florentine steak (€45 for 2), Panna cotta (€6)
Price Range: €€ (€25-40 per person)
Booking: Call ahead or use TheFork app
Location: Piazza del Mercato Centrale
Atmosphere: Romantic courtyard seating

Gelato: La Carraia

Artisan Gelato • Oltrarno

Locals' favorite gelato spot. Creamy, rich, not too sweet. Right by the Arno - grab a cone and walk along the river. Better than the touristy spots near Ponte Vecchio.

Best Flavors: Pistachio, Stracciatella, Salted caramel, Seasonal fruit
Price: €2.50-4 depending on size
Locations: Multiple, main one at Piazza Nazario Sauro
Tip: Go for the medium (you'll want more!)
Perfect After: Dinner walk along the Arno

Gelato: Gelateria dei Neri

Artisan Gelato • Santa Croce

Another top gelato spot near Santa Croce. Their pistachio is legendary. No artificial colors - real ingredients only. Always a line of locals (good sign!).

Must-Try: Pistachio (the best!), Crema, Chocolate, Nocciola
Price: €2.50-4
Location: Via dei Neri 9/11r
Perfect For: Post-Uffizi treat

💡 Florence Insider Tips

🚶 Getting Around

  • Walk everywhere - Florence is tiny and compact!
  • City center is mostly pedestrian-only
  • No need for taxis or public transport
  • Wear COMFORTABLE shoes (cobblestones everywhere)
  • Use Google Maps offline mode

💰 Money-Saving Tips

  • Sandwich from All'Antico Vinaio = €6 lunch
  • Mercato Centrale upstairs = cheap, amazing food
  • Buy wine from grocery stores (€5-8 good bottles)
  • Free walking tours (tip-based) are excellent
  • Refill water at free fountains citywide

⚠️ What to Avoid

  • Restaurants right on Piazza del Duomo (overpriced)
  • Skip the Firenze Card (not worth it for 3 days)
  • Don't buy leather near Duomo (tourist quality)
  • Avoid guided museum tours (do self-guided)
  • Don't skip booking Uffizi/Accademia in advance!

📸 Photo Spots

  • Piazzale Michelangelo at sunset (THE view!)
  • Ponte Vecchio from Ponte Santa Trinita
  • Duomo from Giotto's Bell Tower
  • Boboli Gardens rose garden
  • Via dei Calzaiuoli at sunrise (empty!)

📅 Sample 3-Day Florence Itinerary

Day 1: Renaissance Art

Morning: Arrive from Milan, check in, climb Duomo (book in advance!)

Afternoon: Accademia Gallery to see David, wander San Lorenzo market

Evening: Dinner at Trattoria Mario, gelato walk along the Arno

Hit the big sights while you're fresh! David + Duomo = Florence essentials.

Day 2: Chianti Wine Escape

Full Day: Chianti wine tour - vineyards, tastings, Tuscan lunch, medieval towns

Evening: Return to Florence, casual dinner in Oltrarno, Piazzale Michelangelo sunset

The countryside break is worth it! Rolling hills, wine, and Tuscan cooking.

Day 3: Uffizi & Oltrarno

Morning: Early Uffizi Gallery visit (3+ hours)

Afternoon: Ponte Vecchio, explore Oltrarno artisan shops, Palazzo Pitti gardens

Evening: Pizza at Gustapizza, final gelato, farewell to Florence

Save the Uffizi for when you can dedicate serious time. It's overwhelming (in the best way!).
