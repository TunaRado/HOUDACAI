Slovenia Travel Tips

🚗 Driving in Slovenia

EASY! Roads excellent, signs clear, parking available. Buy vignette (toll sticker) at border or gas station. ~€15 for week.

💶 Money Matters

Slovenia uses EURO (joined EU 2007). No currency exchange! ATMs everywhere. Cash still common at small places.

🍰 Food Scene

Mix of Austrian, Italian, Balkan influences. Try kremšnita (cream cake), potica (nut roll), žganci (buckwheat), štruklji (dumplings)!

🗣️ Language

Slovenian is the official language, but English widely spoken in tourist areas. Younger generation = fluent English!
