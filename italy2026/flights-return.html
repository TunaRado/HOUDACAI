Saying Goodbye to Italy

All good things must end! Your return flight from Rome's Fiumicino Airport (FCO) will likely have one connection in the US or Europe. The journey takes 12-16 hours total. Look for Friday afternoon/evening departures that get you home Friday night or Saturday morning, giving you the weekend to recover from jet lag before work.

🛫 Flight Options from Rome

🇺🇸 Delta (via Atlanta)
$420-580 pp

Typical Route:

  • FCO → ATL: Afternoon departure ~2-4 PM (10-11 hours)
  • Arrive Atlanta: Evening same day (time difference)
  • Layover: 2-4 hours
  • ATL → TPA: Late evening (1.5 hours)
  • Arrive Tampa: Late Friday night

Pros: Direct transatlantic, Atlanta hub is easy, familiar US airline
Cons: Long day of travel
Book At: delta.com or Google Flights

🇺🇸 United (via Newark)
$450-620 pp

Typical Route:

  • FCO → EWR: Morning/midday departure (9-10 hours)
  • Arrive Newark: Afternoon same day
  • Layover: 2-5 hours
  • EWR → TPA: Evening flight (3 hours)
  • Arrive Tampa: Late Friday night

Pros: Competitive pricing, direct transatlantic from Rome
Cons: Newark layovers can be long
Book At: united.com or Google Flights

🇺🇸 American (via Philadelphia or Charlotte)
$480-650 pp

Typical Route:

  • FCO → PHL/CLT: Afternoon departure (9-10 hours)
  • Arrive Philadelphia/Charlotte: Evening same day
  • Layover: 2-4 hours
  • PHL/CLT → TPA: Evening/night flight (2-2.5 hours)
  • Arrive Tampa: Late Friday night or early Saturday

Pros: Multiple options, good service, alliance benefits
Cons: Sometimes requires early morning departure from Rome
Book At: aa.com or Google Flights

🇮🇹 ITA Airways (Non-stop to select US cities)
$520-700 pp

Typical Route:

  • FCO → JFK/MIA: Afternoon departure (9-10 hours)
  • Layover: 2-4 hours in New York or Miami
  • JFK/MIA → TPA: Evening connection
  • Arrive Tampa: Late Friday/early Saturday

Pros: Italian hospitality till the end, good food/wine on board!
Cons: Can be pricier, connection adds time
Book At: ita-airways.com

💰 Return Flight Booking Strategy

⚠️ IMPORTANT: Book Round-Trip vs One-Way

Round-trip tickets are almost always cheaper than booking two one-ways! When you search for outbound flights, look at the total round-trip price. You'll typically save $100-300 per person by booking both directions together.

Timing Tips

  • Afternoon departures from Rome work best
  • Avoid 6 AM departures (you'll hate yourself)
  • Landing late Friday gives you the weekend
  • Or consider Saturday morning arrival for rest

Money Savers

  • Book round-trip with outbound flight
  • Be flexible with Friday vs Saturday return
  • Consider nearby airports for connections
  • Join airline loyalty programs before booking

Don't Forget

  • Allow 3 hours at Rome airport
  • Check baggage allowances
  • Save room for souvenirs!
  • Download movies for the flight home

📅 Your Last Day in Rome

Morning

  • Check out of hotel (store luggage if needed)
  • Final espresso at favorite café
  • Last gelato (yes, at 10 AM!)
  • Quick revisit to favorite spot
  • Buy last-minute souvenirs

Getting to Airport

  • Leonardo Express: €14, 32 min from Termini
  • Taxi/Uber: €50-60 fixed rate, 45 min-1 hour
  • Private Transfer: €60-80 booked in advance
  • Arrive 3 hours before international flight
  • FCO is a big airport - allow time!

At the Airport

  • Check bags, get boarding passes
  • Tax refund desk if you made big purchases
  • Security and passport control
  • Browse duty-free for last-minute gifts
  • One final Italian meal at airport!
  • Board flight with a full heart ❤️

🧳 Packing for the Return Flight

Souvenirs to Pack

  • Wine/limoncello (wrap in clothes!)
  • Olive oil and balsamic vinegar
  • Parmesan, truffle products
  • Italian coffee and chocolates
  • Leather goods from Florence
  • Save room in luggage or bring extra bag!

Customs Allowances

  • $800 duty-free per person
  • 1 liter of alcohol duty-free
  • 200 cigarettes duty-free
  • Declare food items (most allowed)
  • Keep receipts for expensive items
  • No fresh meats/unpasteurized cheese

Flight Home Tips

  • Wear comfortable clothes (you'll be sitting 12+ hours)
  • Download Italian movies for nostalgia
  • Start adjusting back to Tampa time
  • Stay hydrated on the flight
  • Sleep if you can (helps with jet lag)
  • Dream about when you'll return!
