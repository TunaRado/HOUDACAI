Today's Itinerary

8:30 AM - Breakfast

Leisurely morning start

  • Hotel breakfast or nearby café
  • Today is less rushed than previous days
  • Pack: Camera, water

10:00 AM - Borghese Gallery

Rome's finest small art museum - absolute must-see!

  • Tickets: €15 (MUST BOOK WEEKS AHEAD - only 360 people per 2-hour slot!)
  • Book at: galleriaborghese.beniculturali.it
  • Metro A to Spagna, then walk through park (15 min)
  • Or taxi directly to gallery entrance
  • Arrive 15 min early - late arrivals forfeit tickets!
  • Ground Floor - Bernini Sculptures:
  • Apollo and Daphne (her fingers turning to leaves - INCREDIBLE!)
  • The Rape of Proserpina (fingers pressing into marble flesh)
  • David (different from Michelangelo's - action pose)
  • Aeneas, Anchises and Ascanius
  • Upper Floor - Paintings:
  • Caravaggio: Boy with a Basket of Fruit, David with Head of Goliath, Madonna dei Palafrenieri
  • Raphael: Deposition, Lady with a Unicorn
  • Titian: Sacred and Profane Love
  • Canova: Pauline Bonaparte as Venus Victrix (reclining sculpture)
  • Exactly 2 hours - you must exit then
  • Small museum but WORLD-CLASS collection

12:00 PM - Villa Borghese Gardens

Rome's most beautiful park

  • Stroll through 80 hectares of landscaped gardens
  • Lake with rowboat rentals (€3/20 min - fun!)
  • Pincio Terrace - panoramic views over Piazza del Popolo
  • Temple of Aesculapius on the lake
  • Great for photos and relaxation
  • Street vendors for snacks
  • Duration: 60-90 minutes

1:30 PM - Lunch at Pincio

Light lunch with a view

  • Casina Valadier: Elegant café on Pincio terrace (€€€, views!)
  • Or pack a picnic and eat in the gardens
  • Alternative: Walk down to Piazza del Popolo area for lunch
  • Canova Tadolini: Sculptor's workshop turned café (€25-35 pp)

3:00 PM - Free Time / Shopping / Rest

Choose your afternoon adventure

  • Option A: Via del Corso shopping (high street brands)
  • Option B: Return to hotel for siesta
  • Option C: Visit Piazza di Spagna and luxury shopping (Via Condotti)
  • Option D: Explore Jewish Ghetto neighborhood
  • Rest up for tonight's food crawl!

5:30 PM - Jewish Ghetto (Optional)

Historic neighborhood worth exploring

  • Walk through ancient Jewish Quarter
  • See the Great Synagogue
  • Portico d'Ottavia (ancient Roman ruins)
  • Try Boccione bakery - Jewish pastries
  • Famous for carciofi alla giudia (Jewish-style artichokes)
  • Duration: 45-60 minutes

7:00 PM - Trastevere Food Crawl Begins

Eat your way through Rome's foodie neighborhood!

  • Walk or taxi to Trastevere
  • Stop 1 - Supplì: Supplizio - best fried rice balls (€3-4)
  • Stop 2 - Pizza: Ai Marmi (aka "the morgue" - marble tables) (€8-12)
  • Stop 3 - Wine Bar: Enoteca Ferrara, try local wines (€10-15)
  • Walk between spots, explore narrow streets
  • See Piazza di Santa Maria in Trastevere (beautiful church)

8:30 PM - Main Dinner

Sit-down meal at a trattoria

  • Da Enzo al 29: If you didn't go Day 11 (book ahead!)
  • Tonnarello: No reservations, expect wait, worth it
  • Dar Poeta: Pizza + fried appetizers (€20-30 pp)
  • Glass Hostaria: Michelin star, modern (€60-80 pp)
  • Order family-style and share!
  • Try: Carciofi alla romana (Roman-style artichokes)
  • Pasta: Whatever Roman classic you haven't had
  • Secondi: Saltimbocca alla romana (veal with prosciutto)

10:00 PM - Trastevere Nightlife

The neighborhood comes alive at night!

  • Live music in Piazza di Santa Maria
  • Bar hop on Via del Moro
  • Freni e Frizioni: Converted garage, great cocktails + aperitivo
  • Ma Che Siete Venuti a Fà: Craft beer bar (tiny!)
  • Ombre Rosse: Boho café/bar, locals' favorite
  • Gelato from Fior di Luna (back for round 2!)
  • Wander the romantic cobblestone streets

💡 Today's Tips

  • Borghese Booking: CRITICAL - book 2-3 months ahead! Sells out completely!
  • Arrival Time: Be there 15 min early - they won't let you in if late!
  • Two Hours: That's all you get! Use your time wisely.
  • Photography: Allowed (no flash) - take lots of photos!
  • Food Crawl: Pace yourself - small portions at each stop
  • Trastevere: Gets VERY crowded 8-10 PM - that's part of the fun!
  • Reservations: Most Trastevere spots don't take them - be flexible
  • Late Night: Trastevere is safe and lively until 1-2 AM

🎨 Why Borghese is Special

The Cardinal's Collection

  • Built by Cardinal Scipione Borghese (nephew of Pope Paul V) in 1600s
  • He was the Medici of Rome - massive art patron
  • Commissioned Bernini when he was just a teenager!
  • Collected Caravaggio works (often controversial)
  • Small palace = intimate viewing experience
  • Limited visitors = can actually see the art!
  • Each Bernini sculpture tells a story frozen in marble
  • Apollo & Daphne: Capture the exact moment of transformation
  • Proserpina: Marble looks like actual flesh (check out the fingers!)
  • Quality over quantity - every piece is a masterwork

🍝 Trastevere Food Guide

What to eat and where:

  • Supplì: Supplizio - Rome's best fried rice balls
  • Pizza: Ai Marmi or Dar Poeta
  • Pasta: Da Enzo al 29 or Tonnarello
  • Artichokes: Carciofi alla romana or alla giudia (in season)
  • Trapizzino: Pizza dough pocket with filling - Trapizzino original shop nearby
  • Gelato: Fior di Luna or Gelateria alla Scala
  • Cocktails: Freni e Frizioni (also great aperitivo buffet)
  • Wine: Enoteca Ferrara or Spirito DiVino
