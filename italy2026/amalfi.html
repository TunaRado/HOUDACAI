Welcome to the Amalfi Coast - Italy's Coastal Masterpiece

The Amalfi Coast is one of the world's most spectacular coastlines - dramatic cliffs plunge into turquoise Mediterranean waters, pastel villages cascade down mountainsides, lemon groves perfume the air, and winding coastal roads offer jaw-dropping vistas around every curve. This UNESCO World Heritage site includes the iconic towns of Positano, Amalfi, and Ravello, each with its own unique charm. Prepare for Instagram overload, incredible seafood, and coastal magic.

🗺️ Must-Do Amalfi Coast Experiences

🎨 Positano - La Dolce Vita

The Crown Jewel

Positano's pastel buildings cascading down to the sea are iconic. Wander the steep pedestrian streets, shop for linen, relax on the beach, and soak in pure beauty.

Time: Full day or half day

Must Do: Beach club lunch, shop Chiesa di Santa Maria

Tip: Best photos from ferry or water

🥾 Path of the Gods Hike

Spectacular Coastal Trail

One of the world's most beautiful hikes! The Sentiero degli Dei trail offers breathtaking clifftop views. Agerola to Nocelle is the classic route.

Time: 3-4 hours (moderate difficulty)

Distance: 4.5 miles with elevation

Best Time: Morning (cooler, better light)

🌸 Ravello - Gardens in the Sky

Hilltop Paradise

Perched high above the coast, Ravello offers stunning gardens (Villa Cimbrone & Villa Rufolo), classical music venues, and panoramic terraces.

Time: Half day

Entry: €7-10 per villa garden

Must See: Villa Cimbrone's Terrace of Infinity

⛵ Boat Tour Along Coast

See It from the Water

The best views of the coast are from the sea! Take a private or group boat tour, swim in hidden coves, visit grottos, and see Positano from the water.

Time: 3-8 hours (various options)

Cost: €80-200 per person

Stops: Positano, Emerald Grotto, swimming spots

🍋 Limoncello Tasting

Liquid Sunshine

The Amalfi Coast is famous for huge lemons and limoncello. Visit a local producer, taste varieties, and bring bottles home!

Time: 1 hour

Cost: Often free with purchase

Best: Family-run hillside producers

⛪ Amalfi Town & Cathedral

Historic Maritime Republic

The town of Amalfi was once a powerful maritime republic. Visit the stunning Arab-Norman cathedral and explore the charming piazza.

Time: 2-3 hours

Must See: Cathedral, Cloister of Paradise

Vibe: More laid-back than Positano

💡 Amalfi Coast Insider Tips

  • Getting Around: SITA buses connect towns (€2-3), but they're packed in summer. Consider private transfers or ferries for comfort.
  • Ferry is King: Ferries between towns are scenic, relaxing, and avoid the crazy coastal road. Positano-Amalfi €8-10.
  • Base Location: Stay in one town and day trip to others. Praiano or Positano are central. Amalfi is more affordable.
  • Driving: The coastal road is stunning but STRESSFUL. Narrow, winding, buses inches away. Only for confident drivers!
  • Steps Everywhere: Be prepared to climb. Lots of stairs! Wear comfortable shoes. This is not wheelchair accessible.
  • Peak Season Chaos: June-August is PACKED. Shoulder season (May, Sept, early Oct) is ideal - still warm, fewer crowds.
  • Book Ahead: Good restaurants and boat tours fill up fast. Book 2-4 weeks ahead in summer.
  • Beach Clubs: Public beaches are small. Most people go to beach clubs (€15-30 for lounger/umbrella).
  • Dress Code: Swimsuit cover-ups required when not at beach. Many churches/shops enforce this.
  • Sunset Timing: Positano faces west = amazing sunsets. Amalfi faces south = sunrise better.
