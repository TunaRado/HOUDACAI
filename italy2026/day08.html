🚄 Milan → Florence (Firenze)

Train: Frecciarossa high-speed

Duration: 1 hour 50 minutes

Route: Milano Centrale → Firenze S.M.N. (Santa Maria Novella)

Cost: €30-50 per person

Today's Itinerary

9:00 AM - Milanese Breakfast

Last morning in Milan

  • Final Italian breakfast near hotel
  • Hotel checkout by 11 AM
  • Store luggage at hotel if train is later

11:00 AM - To Milano Centrale

Head to the train station

  • Metro to Milano Centrale station
  • Arrive 30 minutes before departure
  • Find platform (binario) on departure boards
  • Grab snacks/water for journey

12:00 PM - Frecciarossa to Florence

Scenic journey through Emilia-Romagna and Tuscany

  • Board high-speed train
  • Watch Tuscan hills roll by
  • See vineyards, medieval towns, cypress trees
  • Train passes through Bologna
  • Arrive Firenze S.M.N. around 2 PM

2:00 PM - Arrive in Florence

Welcome to the birthplace of the Renaissance!

  • Exit Santa Maria Novella station (beautiful 1930s architecture)
  • Florence is small and walkable - most likely walking to hotel
  • 15-20 minute walk to most central hotels

2:30 PM - Hotel Check-In

Hotel Brunelleschi - Tower room with Duomo views!

  • Prime location between Duomo and Ponte Vecchio
  • Built into a medieval tower and Byzantine church
  • Request tower room for panoramic views
  • Drop bags, freshen up, get city map
  • Rest for 30 minutes after journey

3:30 PM - First Glimpse of Florence

Duomo di Firenze - right around the corner!

  • Walk to Piazza del Duomo (5 minutes from hotel)
  • Stand in awe of Brunelleschi's Dome - architectural miracle!
  • See the pink, white, and green marble facade
  • Giotto's Campanile (bell tower) alongside
  • Baptistery with Gates of Paradise doors
  • Take LOTS of photos from every angle
  • Don't enter yet - save for tomorrow's climb!

4:30 PM - Walk to Piazza della Signoria

Florence's main square - outdoor sculpture museum

  • 10-minute walk from Duomo
  • See Palazzo Vecchio (city hall fortress)
  • Replica of Michelangelo's David in front
  • Loggia dei Lanzi - open-air sculpture gallery
  • Perseus by Cellini, Rape of the Sabine Women
  • Watch street performers and artists
  • Sit on the steps and people-watch

5:30 PM - Ponte Vecchio

Florence's iconic medieval bridge

  • Walk down to the Arno River (5 min from Piazza Signoria)
  • Cross the famous Ponte Vecchio (Old Bridge)
  • Browse gold and jewelry shops built into the bridge
  • View from the middle of the bridge looking up/down river
  • Cross to Oltrarno (left bank) neighborhood
  • Bohemian artisan quarter with authentic feel

6:30 PM - Aperitivo Florentine-Style

Find a spot for sunset drinks

  • La Terrazza Rooftop Bar (Hotel Continentale) - Ponte Vecchio views
  • Se·sto on Arno (The Westin) - panoramic terrace
  • Volume - hip bar in Oltrarno
  • Order: Negroni (invented in Florence!) or Chianti wine
  • Watch the sunset paint the city golden

8:00 PM - First Florentine Dinner

Time for legendary bistecca alla fiorentina!

  • Trattoria Zà Zà: Tourist-friendly, good food (€30-45 pp)
  • Il Latini: Family-style, huge portions (€35-50 pp)
  • Perseus: Near Duomo, traditional (€30-40 pp)
  • Must order: Bistecca alla Fiorentina (T-bone steak, priced by weight, €40-60 for two)
  • Start with: Crostini toscani (chicken liver pâté on toast)
  • Pasta: Pappardelle al cinghiale (wild boar ragu)
  • Wine: Chianti Classico or Brunello di Montalcino
  • Note: Bistecca is RARE - no choice! That's the Florentine way

10:00 PM - Evening Stroll

Florence by night is magical

  • Walk back across Ponte Vecchio (beautifully lit)
  • See Piazza della Signoria illuminated
  • Duomo with dramatic night lighting
  • Gelato from Vivoli or Gelateria dei Neri
  • Wander the medieval streets
  • Back to hotel - rest up for art-filled tomorrow!

📍 Florence City Center

💡 Florence Arrival Tips

  • Walking City: Florence center is compact - walk everywhere, no metro needed!
  • Cobblestones: Wear comfortable shoes - lots of uneven medieval streets
  • Bistecca: Ordered by weight (usually 1.2-1.5kg for two), always rare
  • Museum Tickets: Book Uffizi and Accademia online NOW - long lines otherwise
  • Duomo Climb: Book time slot online for Brunelleschi's Dome climb
  • Negroni: Florence invented it! Try at Caffè Casoni or Caffè Giacosa
  • Crowds: June is busy - museums and attractions packed midday
  • Budget: Florence is pricey but worth it - plan for €40-60 pp for dinner

🍝 Florentine Food Essentials

  • Bistecca alla Fiorentina: Massive T-bone steak, chargrilled, always rare
  • Pappa al Pomodoro: Thick tomato and bread soup
  • Ribollita: Hearty bread and vegetable soup
  • Pappardelle al Cinghiale: Wide pasta with wild boar ragu
  • Lampredotto: Cow stomach sandwich (adventurous eaters!)
  • Crostini Toscani: Chicken liver pâté on toasted bread
  • Schiacciata: Flatbread with olive oil and salt
  • Vin Santo + Cantucci: Dessert wine + almond biscotti for dipping

🎨 Tomorrow's Art Preview

Day 9 is Renaissance art immersion! You'll see Michelangelo's David at the Accademia, masterpieces at the Uffizi Gallery (Botticelli's Birth of Venus, works by Leonardo, Raphael, Caravaggio), and climb Brunelleschi's incredible Dome for panoramic Florence views. Book tickets in advance!
