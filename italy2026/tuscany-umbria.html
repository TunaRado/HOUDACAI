The Italy of Your Dreams

Rolling hills, cypress-lined roads, medieval towns perched on hilltops, endless vineyards, and food that will change your life. Tuscany and Umbria are the Italy you've imagined - minus the Venice crowds, plus authentic experiences!

Tuscany - Renaissance & Wine

6 Nights • Chianti, Siena & San Gimignano

Tuscany needs no introduction - it's the poster child for Italian beauty. But beyond Florence (covered separately), the countryside holds medieval towns, wine estates, and landscapes that look painted. This is slow travel at its finest!

🏨 Where to Stay in Tuscany

Agriturismo in Chianti

~$140-180/night

Stay on a wine estate! Agriturismos are working farms offering rooms - authentic Tuscan experience!

  • Surrounded by vineyards
  • Home-cooked dinners available
  • Wine tastings on-site
  • Swimming pool, gardens
  • Examples: Castello di Verrazzano, Fattoria Viticcio

Need rental car to access, but totally worth it!

Siena - Hotel Athena

~$160/night

Rooftop terrace overlooking Siena's terracotta roofs. Perfect base for exploring the town.

  • 15 min walk to Piazza del Campo
  • Parking available (essential in Siena!)
  • Views of the Duomo

San Gimignano - Hotel L'Antico Pozzo

~$180/night

15th-century building in the heart of the medieval town. Stone walls, original frescoes, modern comfort.

Budget Option: Monteriggioni B&B

~$95/night

Stay in a walled medieval village! Monteriggioni is tiny but perfectly preserved. 15 min drive from Siena.

🎯 What to Do in Tuscany

Chianti Wine Region

THE quintessential Tuscany experience!

  • Wine Tasting Tours
    • Visit 2-3 wineries per day
    • Tastings: €15-30 per winery
    • Lunch at estate (€30-50)
    • Book ahead (especially weekends!)
    • Recommended: Castello di Verrazzano, Badia a Passignano, Antinori
  • Scenic Drives
    • SR222 (Via Chiantigiana) - THE iconic Chianti road
    • Greve in Chianti - wine town with great shops
    • Panzano - butcher Dario Cecchini (famous from books/TV!)
    • Radda, Gaiole, Castellina - classic Chianti towns
  • Olive Oil Tastings
    • Often combined with wine tours
    • Learn about harvest, pressing, tasting
    • Buy to bring home (pack carefully!)

Siena - Medieval Masterpiece

  • Piazza del Campo
    • Shell-shaped main square (one of Europe's best!)
    • Site of Palio horse race (July 2 & Aug 16)
    • Torre del Mangia - climb for views (€10)
    • Sit at café and people-watch
  • Siena Cathedral (Duomo)
    • Black & white striped marble exterior
    • Incredible inlaid marble floors
    • Piccolomini Library - Pinturicchio frescoes
    • Entry: €15 (worth every penny!)
  • Walk the Contrade
    • 17 medieval districts, each with symbol/colors
    • Fountains, plaques, rivalry!
    • Get lost in narrow streets
  • Siena Food
    • Pici pasta (thick hand-rolled spaghetti)
    • Panforte (spiced fruit/nut cake)
    • Ricciarelli (almond cookies)

San Gimignano - Medieval Manhattan

  • The Towers
    • 14 medieval towers still standing (was 72!)
    • Skyline unchanged since 1300s
    • Climb Torre Grossa (€9) for panoramic views
  • Piazza della Cisterna
    • Triangular medieval square
    • 13th-century well in center
    • Surrounded by towers & palaces
  • Vernaccia Wine
    • Local white wine (only white DOCG in Tuscany!)
    • Wine shops everywhere for tastings
    • Crisp, refreshing, pairs with everything
  • Gelateria Dondoli
    • World champion gelato!
    • Sergio has won international awards
    • Creative flavors, always a line
    • 100% worth the wait!
  • Tips:
    • VERY touristy (day-trippers from Florence)
    • Go early (before 10am) or late (after 5pm)
    • Stay overnight - town empties at sunset!
    • Park outside walls (€2/hour)

Other Tuscany Highlights

  • Val d'Orcia - UNESCO landscape
    • THE postcard Tuscany (cypress trees, rolling hills)
    • Pienza - perfect Renaissance town
    • Montalcino - Brunello wine capital
    • Montepulciano - hilltop wine town
  • Volterra - Etruscan town
    • Alabaster workshops
    • Roman theater ruins
    • Less crowded than San Gimignano!
  • Cortona - Under the Tuscan Sun town
    • Hilltop charmer near Umbria border
    • Etruscan history, views to Trasimeno lake
    • Frances Mayes made it famous

Umbria - Italy's Green Heart

4 Nights • Assisi, Orvieto & Perugia

Umbria is "Tuscany without the crowds" - similar beauty, hill towns, wine, but more authentic and affordable. Plus spiritual sites like Assisi make it special. The food might even be better (don't tell Tuscans!)

🏨 Where to Stay in Umbria

Assisi - Hotel Sorella Luna

~$120/night

Just outside old town walls, quiet location. Named after St. Francis's "Sister Moon" prayer.

  • 5 min walk to Basilica
  • Parking included
  • Garden terrace with valley views
  • Family-run warmth

Orvieto - B&B Valentina

~$95/night

Charming B&B in historic center, rooftop terrace. Best value in Orvieto!

Perugia - Etruscan Chocohotel

~$110/night

Chocolate-themed boutique hotel (Perugia is chocolate capital!). Fun and central.

Countryside - Agriturismo in Montefalco

~$130/night

Stay in wine country! Montefalco Sagrantino is one of Italy's great reds. Pool, cooking classes available.

🎯 What to Do in Umbria

Assisi - Spiritual & Beautiful

  • Basilica of St. Francis
    • Two-level basilica (Upper & Lower churches)
    • Giotto frescoes in Upper Church (masterpieces!)
    • St. Francis's tomb in crypt
    • FREE entry (donations welcome)
    • Dress modestly (shoulders/knees covered)
    • NO PHOTOS inside
  • Old Town Assisi
    • Pink stone buildings glow at sunset
    • Rocca Maggiore fortress - climb for views (€7)
    • Piazza del Comune - Roman temple remains
    • Wander peaceful medieval streets
  • Hermitage of Carceri
    • St. Francis's hermitage in the woods
    • 2km from town (walk or drive)
    • Incredibly peaceful, beautiful forest
    • FREE
  • Tips:
    • Can get crowded (pilgrimage site)
    • Go early to Basilica
    • Stay overnight - evenings are magical

Orvieto - City on a Cliff

  • Orvieto Cathedral (Duomo)
    • One of Italy's most beautiful facades!
    • Gothic masterpiece in striped marble
    • Signorelli frescoes inside
    • San Brizio Chapel (€5 extra, worth it!)
  • Underground Orvieto
    • City built on volcanic tuff rock
    • 1,200+ caves and tunnels below!
    • Underground tour (€7, 1 hour)
    • Etruscan wells, medieval wine cellars
    • Fascinating!
  • St. Patrick's Well (Pozzo di San Patrizio)
    • 53-meter deep well with double-helix stairs
    • Designed so donkeys wouldn't meet going up/down!
    • Entry: €5
    • Impressive engineering
  • Orvieto Classico Wine
    • Local white wine (crisp, minerality from volcanic soil)
    • Enotechs all over town for tastings
    • Pair with local pecorino cheese

Perugia - Chocolate & Hilltops

  • Historic Center
    • Etruscan walls still standing!
    • Corso Vannucci - main pedestrian street
    • Fontana Maggiore - medieval fountain
    • Palazzo dei Priori - Gothic palace
  • Perugina Chocolate
    • Home of Baci chocolates!
    • Factory tour & museum (€9)
    • Chocolate-making classes available
    • Or just buy them everywhere in town!
  • Collegio del Cambio
    • Perugino frescoes (Renaissance master)
    • Former money-changers guild
    • €5 entry, small but exquisite

Umbrian Wine Towns

  • Montefalco
    • "Balcony of Umbria" - hilltop views
    • Sagrantino wine (bold red, only here!)
    • Smaller than Montalcino, less crowded
    • Multiple wine tastings in town
  • Torgiano
    • Wine museum (€5)
    • Lungarotti winery tours
    • Quiet wine village

Other Umbrian Gems

  • Spoleto - Roman ruins, summer festival, quieter hill town
  • Gubbio - Medieval perfection, Palazzo dei Consoli
  • Lake Trasimeno - Italy's 4th largest lake, boat trips, islands
  • Norcia - Food capital! Prosciutto, truffles, lentils (damaged by 2016 earthquake but rebuilding)

🚗 Practical Tuscany & Umbria Tips

🚗 Getting Around

MUST RENT A CAR!

This region is built for road trips!

Why:

  • Hill towns hard to reach by train
  • Wineries need car access
  • Countryside is THE experience
  • Freedom to stop at views!

Cost: €45-65/day

Pick up in Florence, drop in Rome

Gas: €1.70-2.00/liter

ZTL Zones: Historic centers are restricted! Park outside walls!

💰 Budget

More affordable than major cities!

Daily Costs:

  • Hotel: €90-180/night
  • Meals: €50-80/day
  • Wine tastings: €15-30 each
  • Gas: €20-30/day driving

Save Money: Agriturismo dinners, lunch instead of dinner, free town walks

⏰ Timing

Avoid August! Hot, crowded, many places closed

Best: May-June, September-October

Special Times:

  • October: Truffle season, grape harvest
  • November: Olive oil harvest
  • Spring: Wildflowers!

Siesta: 1-4pm everything closes! Plan around it!

🍷 Wine Wisdom

Tuscany Wines:

  • Chianti Classico - Sangiovese, everyday drinking
  • Brunello di Montalcino - Premium, age-worthy
  • Vino Nobile di Montepulciano - Elegant
  • Vernaccia di San Gimignano - White, crisp
  • Super Tuscans - Modern blends (expensive!)

Umbria Wines:

  • Sagrantino di Montefalco - Bold, tannic red
  • Orvieto Classico - White, minerality
  • Grechetto - Local white grape

🧀 Food Specialties

Tuscany:

  • Bistecca alla Fiorentina - massive T-bone
  • Pici - thick hand-rolled pasta
  • Ribollita - hearty bread soup
  • Panzanella - bread salad
  • Cantucci - almond biscotti with Vin Santo

Umbria:

  • Truffles! (both black and white)
  • Porchetta - roast pork
  • Lentils from Castelluccio
  • Strangozzi pasta
  • Baci chocolates from Perugia

📸 Photo Ops

🗓️ Sample 10-Day Routing

Days 1-2: Chianti Region

Base in agriturismo. Wine tastings, scenic drives, Greve in Chianti, Panzano butcher visit.

Days 3-4: Siena + San Gimignano

Full day in Siena (Cathedral, Campo, Contrade walk). Day trip to San Gimignano.

Day 5: Val d'Orcia

Scenic drive through Pienza, Montalcino. Wine tasting. Overnight in Montepulciano.

Days 6-7: Assisi

Basilica, old town, hermitage. Evening stroll when pilgrims leave.

Day 8: Orvieto

Cathedral, underground tour, wine tasting, relaxed pace.

Days 9-10: Perugia + Montefalco

Explore Perugia chocolate, Sagrantino wine tasting in Montefalco.
