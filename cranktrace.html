What It Is

Your Engine. Any Screen.

CrankTrace is a wireless diagnostic and display interface for late model Yamaha outboard engines. It bridges your engine's onboard data systems to any modern device — smartphone, tablet, or computer — without proprietary hardware or dealership software.

Whether you're running offshore, idling in the bay, or troubleshooting at the dock, CrankTrace gives you a live window into what your engine is actually doing. Temperature, fuel consumption, hours, fault codes — surfaced clearly, in real time, on whatever screen is closest.

Designed for boat owners who know their equipment and want data that matches that level of understanding. No subscriptions. No cloud dependency. Your engine, your data.

Your Data

Every Trip. Logged.

CrankTrace automatically records a full data log for every trip — from the moment you start the engine to the moment you shut it down. Distance run, engine hours, estimated fuel burned, average and top speed — captured in the background without touching anything.

When something feels off with the engine, you'll have the data to back it up. Export a trip summary and hand it to your mechanic instead of trying to explain what it sounded like. Show them the exact RPM, temp, and fuel pressure at the moment the problem occurred.

For the DIY crowd — correlating fuel burn against speed and RPM over time gives you real efficiency numbers for your specific boat, load, and conditions. Not EPA estimates. Not manufacturer specs. Your numbers, your engine, your water.

GPS tracking is optional. Trip distance and engine hours are recorded regardless — location data is only used if you choose to enable it and is never stored on our servers.

Compatibility

Works With What You Have

🚤 Engines

Older Yamaha outboard engines. Tools like YDS exist and work — but they're wired, require a laptop on the dock, and are built for dockside diagnostics, not running the boat. CrankTrace is wireless, runs on your phone, and monitors live while you're underway. Different tool, different job. Specific model year compatibility confirmed at launch.

📱 Devices

Android and iOS smartphones, tablets, and any modern browser-capable device. No proprietary display hardware required.

💻 Platforms

PC and Mac via browser interface. Mobile apps for Android and iOS. All platforms display the same live data stream.

📡 Connection

Wireless. Works offshore, at the dock, or on the trailer. No internet connection required for core functionality.
