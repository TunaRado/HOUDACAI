What is Mindrive?

Mindrive generates binaural beats - an auditory phenomenon that occurs when two different frequencies are played separately to each ear. Your brain perceives a third tone based on the mathematical difference, which can influence brainwave patterns for focus, relaxation, meditation, and sleep.

Features

  • 🎧 Stereo audio testing for proper headphone setup
  • 🧠 Precise binaural beat generation
  • 🎵 Customizable frequency controls
  • 🌊 Modulation effects for dynamic experiences
  • ⏱️ Session timer and presets

⚠️ Headphones required for binaural beats to work properly
