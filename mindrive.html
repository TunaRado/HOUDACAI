What It Is

Sound That Changes Your State

Binaural beats are an auditory phenomenon — when two slightly different frequencies are played separately to each ear, your brain perceives a third tone equal to the difference. That perceived tone influences brainwave activity.

Delta waves promote deep sleep. Theta supports creativity and meditation. Alpha encourages relaxed focus. Beta drives active concentration. Mindrive lets you dial in the exact frequency range you need and hold it there with precision-generated audio.

What sets Mindrive apart is control. Carrier frequency, beat frequency, modulation depth, session length — all adjustable. Pre-built journeys for common goals, or build your own from scratch. Your brain, your settings.
